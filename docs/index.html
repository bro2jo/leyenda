The quest

The List

The Confessors have come for the Faithless at Saint Ysolde's with a list: Darrow first, Hollis second. What stands between them and the hall is Old Mercy, the sanctuary bell: while it rings at dawn and dusk, no blade passes the gate and no name under it is taken down against its will. Marrant has not drawn a blade in a month. He has camped on the lower terrace, kept the cage, and reached into the House by the lever: a clerk in grey who never kneels sent two black slivers up in the House's own letter-satchel, one of them now set in the rock under the row and making the stone grow frost, and holds the almoner's sister at Calden for the second. Banners that are not grey are on the Holloway, a day off.

What he is fighting

The false letter is written and held: the second set, the hall quiet, in the almoner's own hand with every word's purpose told, and in it a brother's questions for his sister, where she is kept and a line in her hand, and one true line. It goes down with the carter and is read at the winch-house. If it is believed, the Confessor waits and the clerk may name the place; if it is caught, the next letter up comes without her mark. The answer at the dawn bell is owed under the Prior's roof.

With him

Maelis Vornefond
Came down to the kennels with bad grace and re-splinted the hound in pine and linen; watched the Captain take his hand off the cold grate, and left without a word.
Ser Hollis Garrowloyal
Had himself carried to the cloister at the noon bell; set two chairs and two wooden swords; taught the Captain to stop looking at the floor and to watch the shoulders, and the bind, a blade held is a blade not swinging; beat him on the collarbone and the wrist; went over backwards in his chair on the wet flags and saved it with his sword planted behind him; called that the lesson; named it the Seated Blade; asked to be hit properly, once, not like a Mender.
Wren Ashdownwarming
Sat on the fountain's rim as notary with her ledger; wrote the Seated Blade under Things the Captain can do, and then again under Things Ser Hollis can do; stopped writing when Hollis asked to be hit.
Ashcivil

The quest

The Road

The Captain of the Ninth has gone down the Thousand Steps on his own legs, having asked the Confessor on the lower terrace to keep his name first on the writ, so the hunt follows him down the Holloway and not the House. With him on the carter’s cart go Hollis on a mule, Maelis with her count and a sword called Patience, Wren with her ledger, and the grey hound; behind them the banner that is not grey is coming up the road, and the Confessor has gone down to meet it.

What he is fighting

The road: the company on the Holloway at the foot of the Steps, the banner that is not grey coming up behind, the Confessor gone down ahead to meet it with the Captain’s name asked for and nothing of what he saw.

The quest

The Road

The Captain of the Ninth has gone down the Thousand Steps on his own legs, having asked the Confessor on the lower terrace to keep his name first on the writ, so the hunt follows him down the Holloway and not the House. With him on the carter’s cart go Hollis on a mule, Maelis with her count and a sword called Patience, Wren with her ledger, and the grey hound; behind them the banner that is not grey is coming up the road, and the Confessor has gone down to meet it.

What he is fighting

The road: the company on the Holloway at the foot of the Steps, the banner that is not grey coming up behind, the Confessor gone down ahead to meet it with the Captain’s name asked for and nothing of what he saw.

With him

Maelis Vornefond
Checked the boy’s hip on Rae’s lap with her eyes on the knight’s frosted hands; told Darrow the boy would walk by morning and nothing else.
Ser Hollis Garrowloyal
Said nothing to the knight and put two fingers in the knee-print after; read the crossroads: forty horse turned south, one light horse shod for speed gone alone down the mill track; got the mill-cart’s man talking; told Darrow to use his words again.
Wren Ashdownfond
Kept the book on the knucklebones at Hollis’s table; wrote down the Captain’s first-cast stone with her mouth open.
Rae Thornefond
Took up the pencil to sign the summer away and was stopped by Darrow’s no; had it out with him in the yard; told him he would stand where she put him or be read in the road; asked whether he had ever let anybody pay for him; took the deciding when he gave it.
Ashcivil
Lay along the warm chimney stones in the hay loft and would not be moved; lifted her lip at Darrow and Hollis.

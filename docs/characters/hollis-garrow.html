Ser Hollis Garrow

Knight-Captain of the Sixth Lance

The Sixth Lancealivemajor
The Sixth Lance

Appearance

as the page shows them

Sixty-one, broad, white-bearded and red in the face, with a voice that carries over the Wend in flood. His right leg is gone above the knee, lost at Harrow Ford. He talks in a sergeant's rhythm, profane and warm, insults doing the work of affection, and calls Darrow "Captain" as if it were a joke between them.

Flat on his back on the cot beside Darrow's, staring at the infirmary ceiling and swearing with great precision, having just heard that his name stands second on the Confessors' list.

Now

as far as the reader knows

He lies in the infirmary hall of Saint Ysolde's, in the cot to Darrow's right, second on the list of the Faithless that the Confessors read out at the inn in Coldmere. Six grey cloaks and a cart are on the Holloway below, and if the snow holds off they will reach the foot of the Steps by tomorrow night.

Story so far

spoiler-free

At Harrow Ford he brought the writ from command that sent the Sixth and the Ninth into the river, and when Darrow pointed at the carts and children behind the clan line he answered that the clans were stupid and told him to mount up. He came out of the Wend without his right leg. On the five days of carts and barge back through the Lowmarch, with the Crown's heralds reading the proclamation of the Faithless from every bell-house, he cursed everything from the surgeon to himself and then went quiet. He has held the cot next to Darrow's at Saint Ysolde's since, mostly drunk, and now the Confessors have come up the Holloway with his name second on their list.

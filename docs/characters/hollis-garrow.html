Ser Hollis Garrow

Of the Sixth Lance

The Sixth Lancealive
The Sixth Lance

Appearance

in brief

Sixty-one, broad through the chest, white-bearded and red in the face, with a voice that carries over the Wend in flood. His right leg is gone above the knee since Harrow Ford; under his cot lies the leg the lay brothers made him, beech and a leather cup, which fits the way a boot fits someone else. He talks in a sergeant's rhythm, profane and warm, with insults doing the work of affection, and he calls Darrow "Captain" and lets the word carry whatever he needs it to.

Now

as far as the reader knows

He lies in the infirmary hall of Saint Ysolde's, second on the list of the Faithless that the Confessors read out at the inn in Coldmere, with Benedek Orrin of his own Sixth on the next cot and the writ from Harrow Ford hidden, still unread, in the cup of the wooden leg under his cot. The Captain is shut in a cell down the hall with Mender Vorne, and nothing comes through that door; as the Interlude ends, it opens. Six grey cloaks and a cart are on the Holloway below, and if the snow holds off they will reach the foot of the Steps by tomorrow night.

Story so far

spoiler-free

At Harrow Ford he brought the writ from command that sent the Sixth and the Ninth into the river, and when Darrow pointed at the carts and children behind the clan line he answered that the clans were stupid and told him to mount up. He came back from Harrow Ford on a cart, missing his right leg above the knee. On the five days of carts and barge back through the Lowmarch, with the Crown's heralds reading the proclamation of the Faithless from every bell-house, he cursed everything from the surgeon to himself and then went quiet. He has had the cot next to Darrow's at Saint Ysolde's since, mostly drunk through the weeks before the Binding, and now six grey cloaks and a censer are on the Holloway below with his name second on their list.

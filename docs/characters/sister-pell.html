Sister Pell

Keeper of the bells of Saint Ysolde's

The House of Mendersalive
The House of Menders

Appearance

in brief

Keeper of the House's bells for fifty years, and so old she might have been cut from the hearthstone: small, wrapped in two shawls, with knuckles swollen like walnuts. She watches a speaker's mouth rather than their eyes, and her voice is a dry rasp, rationed. Her glass stands in the bell tower.

Chapter 1 · Scene 3 — The Law of Bells · Saint Ysolde's

Wrapped in two shawls by the fire at Mother Ione's council, watching the Prior's mouth; saying the sanctuary law has lapsed twice, both for snow, never on her rope, and that she will ring Old Mercy as long as it takes.

Now

as far as the reader knows

Keeper of the House's bells, asked by Mother Ione whether she will ring Old Mercy every dawn and every dusk for as long as the grey cloaks care to sit at the foot of the Steps. Fifty years she has rung the bells; she told the Prior to ask the bell if it was tired.

Story so far

spoiler-free

Her glass in the bell tower is where the grey cloaks were first seen on the Holloway. At Mother Ione's council in the warming room she sat by the fire in two shawls and said the sanctuary law has lapsed twice, both for snow, never on her rope; asked whether she would ring Old Mercy dawn and dusk for as long as the grey cloaks wait, she said she had rung the bells fifty years, and the Prior could ask the bell if it was tired.

Story so far

spoiler-free

Sister Pell has not yet appeared in the Chronicle. She was named once, on the morning the first snow came to Saint Ysolde's, when Wren Ashdown came into the infirmary hall out of breath with her ledger held to her chest: from the bell tower, through Sister Pell's glass, Wren had seen six riders and a cart on the Holloway road, grey cloaks, and a censer smoking in the snow. Hollis Garrow named them Confessors. Afterward Wren went back up to the bell tower.

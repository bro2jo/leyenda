Appearance

in brief

Nineteen years old, the youngest of the Ninth Lance. The chronicle gives him no face and no colors: only his age, his place at Darrow's left in the crossing, and his singing, which was bad and did not stop. He rode his own horse into the Wend at Harrow Ford.

Story so far

spoiler-free

He rode with the Ninth Lance at Harrow Ford, at Darrow's left, when the Sixth and the Ninth were sent to clear the ford. Halfway across, the Grace went out of the Ninth: horses stumbled, lances dipped, and Tobin went under his own horse in the current. Darrow came off his mare and turned toward him, and on that turn the knee went. Nineteen of the Ninth came back from Harrow Ford.

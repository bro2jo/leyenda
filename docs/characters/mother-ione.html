Story so far

spoiler-free

Mother Ione has not yet appeared in the Chronicle. On the morning the first snow came to Saint Ysolde's, Wren Ashdown came into the infirmary hall out of breath with news from the bell tower: six riders and a cart on the Holloway, grey cloaks, a censer smoking in the snow, and Mother Ione had been told. There is a list, read out at the inn in Coldmere and passed from a carter to the lay brothers to the kitchen, so that now everyone knows: the names of the Faithless at Saint Ysolde's, the ones the riders have come to bring down to kneel. When Wren had said her piece, Maelis Vorne went to the Prior.

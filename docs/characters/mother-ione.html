Appearance

in brief

The Prior of Saint Ysolde's, in her seventies: small and very upright, white hair cropped close under a plain linen coif, a soft round face creased like a winter apple, and pale eyes that count everyone in a room in turn. Gentle in face and manner, with iron under it. In the warming room she has the one good chair by the fire and offers it to nobody.

Story so far

spoiler-free

When the grey cloaks were sighted on the Holloway, the news went first to Mother Ione. She called a council in the warming room off the cloister: Maelis, Sister Pell, Rae Thorne who had heard the list read, and Darrow, whose name is first on it. She asked how it was read, had the law of Old Mercy said aloud for the Captain, and asked Sister Pell whether she would ring it dawn and dusk for as long as the grey cloaks wait. The council broke with nothing decided aloud, and she called Darrow back to ask what she ought to know before she decides. At the next dawn bell she came to the foot of Darrow's cot: the winch-brothers had taken his far side of the mountain down to the valley, Old Mercy had not rung and would not, for nobody, and when the grey cloaks are at the bars he is to tell Wren when. In the hour before dusk she stood before the gate and let the Confessor read; she kept him waiting at the wicket for the bell as long as the law of guests allowed, and when his men came through and the bell rang late, she gave him the law's other half, that no name under it is taken down against its will. When Darrow gave his name at the bars, the Confessor thanked her for her candour, and she asked Darrow only whether the shoulder was hurt.

Appearance

in brief

The Prior of Saint Ysolde's, in her seventies: small and very upright, white hair cropped close under a plain linen coif, a soft round face creased like a winter apple, and pale eyes that count everyone in a room in turn. Gentle in face and manner, with iron under it. In the warming room she has the one good chair by the fire and offers it to nobody.

Now

as far as the reader knows

Prior of Saint Ysolde's, with the grey cloaks a day from the foot of the Steps. She has heard how the list was read, had the law of Old Mercy said aloud, and asked Sister Pell whether she will ring it dawn and dusk for as long as they wait. She has not yet said whether it will ring; she has asked Darrow what she ought to know first, and what the lay brothers should tell the valley.

Story so far

spoiler-free

When the grey cloaks were sighted on the Holloway, the news went first to Mother Ione. She called a council in the warming room off the cloister: Maelis, Sister Pell, Rae Thorne who had heard the list read, and Darrow, whose name is first on it. She asked how it was read, had the law of Old Mercy said aloud for the Captain, and asked Sister Pell whether she would ring it dawn and dusk for as long as the grey cloaks wait. The council broke with nothing decided aloud, and she called Darrow back to ask what she ought to know before she decides.

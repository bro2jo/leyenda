Ysra Tal

the Needle

The Crown

Only what the story has shown so far, through Chapter 8.

Appearance

Slight and very upright, her hair cropped short as a page’s and a thin old scar running up from her right brow, in a dark coat cut close for riding with six dark buttons down its front and gloves of thin dark leather that fit like skin. She sits a long-legged horse without shifting, keeps her hands quiet, and moves on ice as if it had been laid down for her.

Now

Last seen Chapter 7 · The choice — Counting Breaths · The marsh

On the west bank of the Wend by the marsh with four riders, watching.

Story so far

Sent from Calden to bring in the captain of the Ninth alive and not told why, Ysra Tal lost a day to the carter’s lying road, watched the company from a distance, and summoned him under the forms to the weir. Her one question was why he would not kneel. At the noon bell her footwork found a man who would not move, and she went down on the ice with his point in her shoulder, wanting a reason for the first time.

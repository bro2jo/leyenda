The Sixth Lance

Only what the story has shown so far.

Appearance

Not yet thirty, a knight of Hollis Garrow's own Sixth, on the cot next to his in the infirmary hall. A broad, open face that looks younger than its years: freckles across the nose and cheeks, ears that stand out a little, brown eyes under brows that lift in the middle as if an apology were always ready, thick dark-brown curls gone damp and flattened by the pillow, and a week's brown beard he has not been able to shave. A boar spear went through his shoulder at the ford and a fever came after it; the fever sits red and high on his cheeks, and the shoulder is wrapped like a parcel and tied with cord. He lies with his hands folded on the blanket and watches, with the eyes of a man who still feels a warmth in his chest, or says he does.

Now

Last seen Chapter 3 · Between — The Even Bells · Saint Ysolde's

Lying Hollowed in the Grey Cot. The Captain and Hollis keep his bells for him now, in turns.

Story so far

A knight of Hollis Garrow's Sixth, wounded at Harrow Ford, who says his oath at every bell. On the night of the gate his name was not read, and he took it for mercy and said good night to the Captain. At the dawn bell of the feast he did not say his oath: he lay breathing with his eyes open and frost on his mouth, Hollowed, and the Mender took a chip of black glass from under his tongue. He lies in the Grey Cot under the far cots' blanket with Hollis's hand on his; the House has heard that he said his oath at the first three bells of that night, and not at the fourth.

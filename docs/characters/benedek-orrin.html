The Sixth Lance

Appearance

in brief

Not yet thirty, a knight of Hollis Garrow's own Sixth, on the cot next to his in the infirmary hall. A boar spear went through his shoulder at the ford and a fever came after it; the shoulder is wrapped like a parcel. He lies with his hands folded on the blanket and watches, with the eyes of a man who still feels a warmth in his chest, or says he does.

Now

as far as the reader knows

On the cot next to Hollis Garrow's in the infirmary hall of Saint Ysolde's, feverish from a spear wound at the ford, with his eyes shut and his oath on his lips. Hollis has told him that nobody is taking anyone up eleven hundred steps in the snow, and that they will be let kneel if the Confessors do come up.

Story so far

spoiler-free

A knight of the Sixth Lance who came back from Harrow Ford with a boar spear wound through the shoulder and a fever after it, and has the cot next to Hollis Garrow's at Saint Ysolde's. He says his oath at every bell. On the morning the grey cloaks were seen on the Holloway he asked Hollis what it meant to be second on the list, offered to carry him down the Steps himself if they would let him, and was told he couldn't carry a bucket, which made him smile. Hollis told him to say his oath and shut his eyes, and he did.

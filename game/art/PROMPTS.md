## How every prompt is built (for a new one)

The wording follows the prompt that made the Chronicle's own portraits, so the game's pictures sit beside them. In this order:

1. **The kind of picture first.** `Fantasy RPG character art, full-body figure:` · `Fantasy RPG creature art, full body:` · `Fantasy RPG card illustration, a close view of one clear action:` · `Fantasy RPG environment art:` · `Fantasy RPG character portrait, tight shoulders-up headshot:`
2. **The subject, in plain words.** Age, build, face, clothes with their colours, what is in the hands, the pose. Only what a picture can show; one choice, never "either… or".
3. **The look.** `Painterly semi-realistic video game character art, visible brushwork, grounded dark fantasy, muted medieval palette, realistic skin texture, natural asymmetry, soft daylight.` Where no face is the subject (a card, a place, an animal): `Painterly semi-realistic video game art, visible brushwork, grounded dark fantasy, muted medieval palette.`
4. **Kept out.** `No caricature, no glamour styling, no exaggerated beauty, no flashy armor. Not a photo or 3D render.`
5. **The format.** A figure: `Vertical 2:3 image. One figure only, whole body with a margin all round, turned three-quarters toward the left side of the image. Background: flat pure blue (#0000FF) filling the whole image, like a blue screen. No ground, no shadow, no text. Nothing blue on the figure.` A card: `4:3 landscape. Softly blurred background. No text, no border, no frame.` A portrait for the map: `Eye-level centered square headshot, shoulders visible, turned slightly to the left, space all round the head for a circular crop. No text, no border.`

A figure also says `A distinct, individual face, plain rather than handsome.` and `Match the painting style of the attached image.` Foes face left, friends face right. No prompt runs over 150 words.

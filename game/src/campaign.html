The Line and the Hand

The Chronicle, played. Eleven missions, from the morning at Harrow Ford to the night on the mill bridge, each one a thing the page has shown him do. He starts with the Grace in him and loses it; after that his reach on the board follows the Knots, and his cards carry the rank each Art had in that chapter.

How it differs from a run

  1. Every mission has its own aim, written under the board: reach a tile, hold for so many turns, walk a stretch, stay on a stone. There is no captain to bring down and no camp.
  2. He fights from where he is put. In the House of Menders he moves one step or none. A lit ring marks where someone has to get to; a green one, what must be kept.
  3. Not every opponent is a man. A river, a stair or the cold is fought as a clash: what you deal is ground covered, and its answer is what it costs him.
  4. The deck is the chapter's. No spoils, no buying. Whoever stands beside him lends a card.
  5. Three stars a mission: one for finishing it, two for doing it the way the page did.

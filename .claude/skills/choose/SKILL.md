# /choose

1. `git pull --rebase origin main`, then `python3 engine/saga.py now`. Find the open choice: a **micro-choice** (the `Micro:` line; the numbered list at the end of a daily scene, "What does Darrow say/do?") or the chapter's **climax choice** (`saga/NOW.md`; the list under the climax). If nothing is open, say so; a bare number then is not a choice.
2. Match $ARGUMENTS to an option. If he invents his own option, accept it if it fits the world and his sheet. A free-form action may need a roll (`python3 engine/darrow.py roll ch<NN>-<slug> --stat … --dc … [--prof]`).
3. Gated options: a stat gate (`*[Resolve 10]*`) is checked against `python3 engine/darrow.py sheet --json`; a Bearing gate (`*[Guile 1]*`, `*[Guile 4]*`) against `python3 engine/saga.py bearing show` (the number is the axis value toward that pole; a lean is ≥ 4). If he doesn't meet it, say so in-world and offer the alternatives, or offer to spend Inspiration if the option allows (`python3 engine/darrow.py inspire --reason "…"`).
4. **The ledger entry** (one, terse; schema in `saga/bible/_gm/design.md` §2): `id` `c<NN>.<n>`, `made {chapter, scene, date}`, `chose`, `now` (what changes today: `approval` ±5 to ±15 for a climax, ±1 to ±3 for a micro, bigger only for a betrayal or a sacrifice; `bearing` on the option's one axis, ±1 micro / ±2–3 climax / ±4 betrayal or sacrifice; `flags` **by the names in `saga/state/_gm/plan.json → flags`** (a new flag must be listed in the arc's `## flags`); `factions`), and `due[]` (what falls due later: `when`, `weight`, `what`). Then `python3 engine/saga.py add '<json>' --witnessed <ids of the companions who saw it or will hear of it>`: it applies approval, Bearing, flags and factions and adds the witnesses' ±2 for a landing on their preferred pole. **Never edit approval or Bearing by hand.** Use `--dry-run` first if unsure.
5. Only then record it in `saga/state/world.json → choices[]` **with Edit, never Write**: `{"chapter": N, "scene": "climax" | "<scene key>", "kind": "climax" | "micro", "option": K, "text": "…", "date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "ledger": "c<NN>.<n>", "by": "darrow" | "bearing"}`. If the Bearing crossed a line (a new lean or a named pole), the epithet or the new way people address him must be spoken on the page in the text you write next.
6. **Consequence placement.**
   - **Climax choice:** if `python3 engine/saga.py now` still shows the climax's chapter at stage `climax`, `python3 engine/saga.py plan set stage=choice` (the engine refuses it at any other stage); if the next chapter is already open (the normal case after a checkpoint), **do not touch the stage**; then write the immediate consequence, **100–250 words** appended to the chapter file under `### Choice — <title>`. A climax choice still open at the second close of the next chapter is answered by its planned `default` (`plan climax` prints it), recorded `by: "bearing"`, and its block is written then. Companions react in their own voices; approval changes show in what they say, never as numbers. Any `due` item you pay off here: `python3 engine/saga.py fire ID --where chNN:climax`.
   - **Micro-choice:** `python3 engine/saga.py plan micro close --option K` (`--by bearing` when Darrow answered for himself by the planned default). **No block, no heading**: the consequence is folded into the opening of the next scene when that day closes.
7. Update `saga/state/_gm/threads.md` and `saga/NOW.md` (the climax's open choice leaves it; the choice may bend the next chapter's slots: `python3 engine/saga.py plan slot N key=value …` (plan, kind, beat or quest+stage, micro as JSON), then `saga.py check`; or at the next `plan chapter open`).
8. **The cast and the site** (climax choice only; a micro's consequence is handled with its scene): the companions who reacted get their `last_seen`, `last_seen_doing`, `now` and `appearances` updated (the consequence's anchor is `#choice`); new `known_facts` for anything the consequence revealed; approval and Bearing show on the site by themselves, in words; `saga/state/places.json` for a new place and `world.json → location.place` if the consequence moved him. Then `python3 engine/saga.py check` and `python3 engine/build_site.py` (both must pass).
9. Commit and push: `git add -A && git commit -m "choice: <summary>" && git push origin HEAD:main`.

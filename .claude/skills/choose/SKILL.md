# /choose

1. `git pull --rebase origin main`, then `python3 engine/saga.py now`: the open **small choice** (the `Open micro` line; the numbered list at the end of a daily scene) or the **climax choice** (the `OPEN CHOICE` line). If nothing is open, say so: a bare number is then a quantity, and a Darrow action is `/play`.
2. Match $ARGUMENTS to an option. An option of his own is accepted when it fits the world and his sheet (a roll if it needs one: `python3 engine/darrow.py roll …`). Gates: a stat gate (`*[Resolve 10]*`) against `python3 engine/darrow.py sheet --json`, a Bearing gate (`*[Guile 1]*`, `*[Guile 4]*` for a lean) against `python3 engine/saga.py bearing show`; unmet, say so in-world and offer the alternatives, or Inspiration if the option allows (`python3 engine/darrow.py inspire --reason "…"`).
3. **The ledger entry**, one and terse (schema `saga/bible/_gm/design.md` §2): `id` `c<NN>.<n>`, `made {chapter, scene, date}` (the engine fills what you leave out), `chose`, `now` (`approval` ±1 to ±3 for a small choice, ±5 to ±15 for a climax, more only for a betrayal or a sacrifice; `bearing` on the option's one axis, ±1 small / ±2–3 climax / ±4 betrayal or sacrifice; `flags` by their names in `plan.json → flags`, a new one through `plan flag --new name=false` and the arc's `## flags`; `factions`), and `due[]` (what falls due later: `when`, `weight`, `what`). Then, in one step:
   `python3 engine/saga.py choose --option K [--by darrow|bearing] [--text "the option as the page has it"] '<entry>' --witnessed <ids of the companions who saw it or will hear of it>`
   It records the entry (the witnesses' ±2, the Downstream due item for every flag it sets), writes the `choices[]` row the site reads, and closes the choice. `--by bearing` with no `--option` takes the planned default when Darrow answered for himself. `--which micro|climax` when both are open (the small one resolves first). `--dry-run` is `saga.py add`'s; use `add --dry-run` to test an entry.
4. **The consequence.**
   - **A small choice:** nothing to write. It is folded into the opening of the next scene; the brief carries it.
   - **A climax choice:** `python3 engine/saga.py brief --choice` (the choice, its entry and what it owes, the arc's Downstream line, the people, the format). Write the `### Choice — <title>` block in the climax's own chapter file (the previous chapter's file once the next is open): the moment the choice lands, usually 100–250 words; companions react in their own voices by their under-pressure lines; approval shows in what they say, never as numbers; if the Bearing crossed a line, the epithet or the new way people address him is spoken here. **A Book's last climax:** the block is the crossing itself, written to `python3 engine/saga.py arc tN.<road>` in the light of what he chose, as long as the crossing needs; then `python3 engine/saga.py plan beat tN status=done` and `plan companion arrive ID` for anyone the road put beside him. Then the reread (`style.md` §4) and `python3 engine/saga.py record '{"key": "chNN:choice", …}'` (the skeleton is in the brief; `"fired"` for every due item the block paid).
   - **A beat choice** (a small choice marked `beat: true`): recorded like a small choice with the climax's weights; the tier's flag is set by a second entry at that chapter's climax.
5. If the choice bends the next chapter's planned slots: `python3 engine/saga.py plan slot N key=value …`, then `check`; or at the next `/chapter`.
6. `python3 engine/darrow.py ship -m "choice: <the option as the page has it>"` (reader-safe: never a flag or a GM name).

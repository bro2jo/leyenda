# /checkpoint: the week closes, the chapter climaxes

Do the Ledger fully before writing any story.

## A. Close out the week
1. `git pull --rebase origin main`.
2. If Saturday (or any earlier day this week) isn't closed, close it now (the `/log` close procedure, including its scene). A Saturday with nothing logged at all (no daily, nutrition or food row) is missed: `python3 engine/saga.py plan done N --skipped`; its spine content folds into the climax (Friday's slot is never promoted).
3. `python3 engine/darrow.py chapter-close --date <last Saturday>` (always pass the Saturday that just ended, e.g. `--date 2026-10-10`). This freezes that week's score and tier as `chapter N` and prints the breakdown. Note **N** and the **tier**. From here the dice (`--chapter N`), the road and the week's XP bonus read the frozen tier; a late log still moves totals and the live score, and `sync` says so in a note, but never the tier.
4. **A week with nothing logged at all** (no daily, nutrition or food row on any of its seven days; `python3 engine/darrow.py week --date <a day in it>` shows an empty table) is still a chapter: the calendar numbers chapters, and `chapter-close` scores it (a Setback, counted toward the Book's road: the world moved without him, never a lecture). Oldest first: `chapter-close --date <its Saturday>`; create its chapter file (`# Chapter N — Title`, an epigraph, one `### Interlude — Title` of 300–550 words: the world moving in his absence, another POV, no climax, no choice, one planned world move from the arc's open threads); `python3 engine/saga.py plan chapter open --number N '{"week_start": "<its Sunday>", "question": "<one line>", "slots": [], "world_moves": [], "climax": {"plan": "", "checks": [], "options": [], "default": null}}'` (a chapter with no slots is a **cutaway chapter**: `check` asks nothing of its slots, `plan done` is not needed); world.json (`chapter`, `chapter_title`, `chapter_file`, `scenes[]` with the interlude, a `recaps` line), the cast for anyone in the interlude, `check`, `build`; then go on to the current week. An open climax choice carries across the cutaway and resolves on its normal schedule in the next real chapter. Without `--date` the engine picks the most recent completed week that isn't closed yet and refuses a week still in progress unless `--force` is given; `--force` is also needed to redo a closed week.

## B. The Ledger recap (real only)
1. `python3 engine/darrow.py recap --date <last Saturday> --write`. It creates `real/checkpoints/<Sunday>.md` (named for today, the Sunday after the week) from the template, with fixed headings, and fills its numbers block: the day table, every macro and micro average vs target or reference with days in range, weight vs last week and the guide's rule, loaded days → next-morning responses, floor, swelling grades, sessions vs plan, what was measured, and a flags line. Quote those numbers; never recompute them. A log that arrives later for that week: rerun the same command (it rewrites only the block).
2. Write the meaning under each heading of that file, replacing the comment there. Read the state file a section at a time (`python3 engine/darrow.py doc state <section>`) and this week's `real/visits/` files; never open the plans whole.
   - **Nutrition:** what the flagged nutrients mean and the foods behind them (`food list DATE`), fish (omega-3 days in range), one or two fixes for next week.
   - **Weight:** the trend. If the engine printed the ⚠ falling line, say so plainly and suggest telling the surgeon/PCP. If the guide's rule moves the target, update `real/config.json → nutrition_targets` with the source and date.
   - **Rehab:** the loaded days and their mornings, the swelling trend, progressions made and whether each followed the one-change rule (`show ex DATE`).
   - **Add-on and conditioning:** upper 2 of 2? (decides block advancement: `doc addon 6`); bike; accessory/power.
   - **Sport:** sessions vs plan (3, or the minimum-viable 2 + 1), the phase's throwing and the pivot ladder's rung (a Knot tied this week opens the next phase's throwing: update `config.json → sport.stage`, `stage_since`, `stage_evidence`), the guide's weekly question. Every fourth week (guide Weeks 4, 8, 12, then monthly) the month check: the 1–5 ratings as `review` rows and the guide's seven review questions (`doc sport reviews`).
   - **PT and measurements:** each visit with its `real/visits/` file; the numbers measured (`measures`); questions answered and still open.
   - **Gates:** the next phase gate (`real/config.json → rehab.next_gate`), criterion by criterion, with evidence. **If a gate was passed with PT/surgeon clearance or measured criteria this week:** `python3 engine/darrow.py knot tie N --date … --evidence "…"`, update `rehab.current_phase` / `phase_entered` / `next_gate`, and run `sync`.
   - **Program — changes and decisions:** what changed in the program or targets, and why.
   - **Next week:** the plan, day by day, with doses (or a pointer to the state file's program section if nothing changed).
3. **The state file holds the present only.** If the program changed (a visit's items folded in, a new phase, a new add-on block), write a new `real/state/ACL_Recovery_State_<Sunday>.md` in the same structure (`doc state` prints the outline): "Program set" and "Last reviewed" this Sunday, "Supersedes" naming the old file, no "Newer than this file" line for visits it folded in. Then `git mv` the old one into `real/state/archive/`. If nothing changed, update only its "Last reviewed" line and whatever status moved (phase status, instructions in force, patterns, milestones). The week's review never goes in the state file; it is the checkpoint's.
4. Update the top section of `real/NOW.md` for the new week: plan in force (pointing at the state file), next PT visit and its questions, the gate, flags and notes, open items. No counts there; the engine block owns them.
5. Give him a ≤15-line summary in chat.

## C. The climax (story only)
If `python3 engine/saga.py now` shows an open micro, resolve it first by its default (`/choose` with `--by bearing`); the climax never opens with a micro pending. Then `python3 engine/saga.py plan set stage=climax` **first**, so the climax-targeted consequences count as due, and only then `python3 engine/saga.py now` (**read the RULE ARMED / near lines and any DUE NOW or OVERDUE item before writing**; `python3 engine/saga.py due` with no arguments lists everything due or overdue now, including owed world moves; `--at chNN:climax` is an exact-target lookup only). Read `saga/bible/style.md`, `python3 engine/saga.py plan climax` (the chapter's question, climax plan, checks, options, default, world moves left; never open `plan.json` whole), `python3 engine/saga.py arc <the chapter's beat id>` (its per-tier climax sketch), `saga/state/_gm/threads.md`, `saga/bible/cast.md` and the `_gm/characters.md` entries of everyone in the climax (their *Under pressure* lines; a newcomer's too, from the not-yet-on-the-page section), and the chapter so far.

**Roads.** The tier also scores the Book's road (`python3 engine/saga.py route show`: Triumph +1, Setback −1; the road is decided only at the Knot). Write the climax so that it answers the chapter's **question** (the first line `plan climax` prints) in the tier's shape; a skipped Saturday's spine content and any owed world move open it.

**The tier sets the shape of the outcome:**
- **Triumph:** Darrow's plan works; a clean win, plus something extra (an ally, a secret, an item).
- **Hard-won:** he wins, and it costs something real (an injury in-story, a relationship strained, a resource lost).
- **Costly:** partial: he gets one thing he wanted and loses another.
- **Setback:** the enemy gains ground. Darrow survives, and the reversal sets up the next chapter. Never humiliating, never a lecture.

**The dice decide the details.** 2–4 checks at the moments of real stakes:
`python3 engine/darrow.py roll ch<NN>-<slug> --stat <might|vigor|finesse|resolve> --dc <8–20> --prof --chapter <N>`
(`--prof` when an Art or trained skill applies; `--art <id>` adds an Art's rank by its `rules.json` id: an **Insight** check is `--stat resolve --prof --art wardens_eye`, and a Resolve check against fear, haste or temptation takes `--art menders_patience`; `--adv` when an Art grants advantage; `--dis` when the situation is against him.) The engine prints the chapter's dice line first, in the style guide's form (`` `[MIGHT · DC 13]` d20 **14** +2 = **16** — *Success* ``); paste that first line into the chapter exactly as printed, on its own line, then narrate the result. The second line is only a note for you. If he has Inspiration and a roll fails at a key moment, you may offer the reroll as part of the choice rather than spending it yourself.

Fire the callbacks that fit the climax (at most one) and void the stale ones with a reason (`python3 engine/saga.py due --later`). Write **900–1,800 words** under `## Climax — <Title>` in the current chapter file, then the reread (`saga/bible/style.md` §4) before anything below. Fire what you paid off (`python3 engine/saga.py fire ID --where chNN:climax`; `void` what the climax made impossible). When his answer comes, `/choose` records it, and `saga.py add` gives every flag the choice sets a due item for the arc's Downstream line, aimed at the next chapter: the next chapter's first spine slot honours it (`plan next --full` prints the line), and you `fire` it there. End with **the choice** (2–3 options from the climax plan, gated options marked per `style.md`, stat gates and Bearing gates alike); the open choice goes in `saga/NOW.md`. When he answers, `/choose` records it; it carries across the first two scenes of the next chapter and then resolves to the climax's planned `default`, `by: bearing`.

**If a Knot tied this week** (`darrow.py knot tie` ran in step B): the chapter is the Book's transition. `python3 engine/saga.py route decide --book N` (it refuses while a listed chapter is unclosed, unless `--road … --why …`), then `python3 engine/saga.py due` again: items aimed at `transitionN` fall due only once the road is decided and `transition_chapter` is set; fire them in the transition climax. Then `python3 engine/saga.py arc tN.<road>` and write the climax in that shape (in all three the Knot ties and nobody is humiliated; only the circumstances, company and carry-forward differ). Afterwards `python3 engine/saga.py plan beat tN status=done`; core beats still `planned` fold into it in order.

## D. Turn the page
1. Create the next chapter file `saga/chronicle/<NN+1>-<slug>.md` with its heading and an epigraph. If a Knot tied this week, the new chapter opens the next Book (`# BOOK N — <TITLE>` title line; `python3 engine/saga.py arc bookN.question`). The build accepts the new chapter while it has no scene yet; the Now page shows the last chapter until scene 1 is written.
2. **Plan the chapter:** Start from `python3 engine/saga.py plan chapter template --week_start <Sunday>` (a canonical skeleton), fill it in a scratch file, then: if a Knot tied this week, `python3 engine/saga.py plan book open N+1` first (it sets the position's Book, seeds `route.bookN+1` and the Book's core beats from the arc, and marks the old Book's unwritten beats folded), then `python3 engine/saga.py plan chapter open --number <N+1> '<json>'` (shape in `saga/bible/_gm/design.md` §5.3–5.4; the number must be the next one, one chapter per calendar week; it refuses while a micro is open): a one-line `question`; 7 slots Sun–Sat with `kind` (≥ 2 `spine`, Saturday always spine, ≤ 1 `interlude`, two quest slots marked `float: true`), each with its `beat` or `quest`+`stage`, a one-line `plan` from `saga.py arc <id>` and a one-line `turn` (who can, must, believes, risks or chooses what by the end that they could not at the start, and what Darrow attempts; `plan chapter open` warns about a slot without one and `check` notes it); a slot that reveals a truth only when that thread shows two scene keys in the *On the page* column of `saga/state/_gm/threads.md`, otherwise the slot plants; **the devices** by the pacing rule (design §5.6): at most one per slot, none on neighbouring days, at most two of the three kinds in the chapter (one challenge at most, two invitations at most) and no kind the last chapter carried, each with its fields (`challenge`, `invite`; a project stage as a quest slot of the Book's project, never in two chapters running), the invitations drawn from their people's *Under pressure* lines; and every device gated by the page (design §5.6): a challenge uses only an Art the chronicle has named, an invitation comes only from someone with a character file who is alive, who is where Darrow is by their `last_seen.place` (or whom the slot's scene brings to him), whose standing rule in `plan.json → invite_rules` is met, with a `why_now` from their want and the night the spine has made, and carrying only what that person knows, a project's place is on the page, and a quest with a `gate` (`plan quest ID gate='…'`, from the arc's *can run when*) waits until it is met; `plan chapter open` warns, `check` refuses; micro-choices in non-adjacent slots touching ≥ 2 axes, each with its `default`; `world_moves[]`; the `climax` plan with `checks`, `options` and a `default` (the option the highest-approval companion present would pick). A slot's `plan` may name one entry of the Annals (`lore:<id>`) where a touch fits its content, never more than one per slot and none in the climax plan's action; most slots name none. `required` quests first; never more than two quests live; the chapter touches ≥ 1 open thread. The choice he just made bends these slots (afterwards, `python3 engine/saga.py plan slot N key=value …` edits one planned slot; `--force` for a written or skipped one).
3. `python3 engine/saga.py plan beat <the chapter's beat> status=done` (the climax answered it); the next chapter's first spine slot opens the next beat. Update `saga/state/world.json` by Edit: chapter number/title/file, `scenes` reset, `last_beat`, `current_quest`, `current_struggle`, a `recaps` line (never approval). Then `saga/state/_gm/threads.md` (plants and payoffs), `codex.md`, and the narrative top of `saga/NOW.md` (the open choice goes there).
4. **The cast and the site:** files in `saga/characters/` for anyone new on the page in the climax (contract: `saga/characters/README.md`); for everyone in it, `last_seen`, `last_seen_doing`, `now`, `appearances`, `known_facts` (with `evidence` where the README requires it), `status` if someone fell or was taken, and `story_so_far` for the chapter just closed; `saga/state/places.json` for new places; `world.json → location.place`, `current_quest.on_the_page`, and a one-line spoiler-free `recaps["<new chapter slug>"]`. A tied Knot shows up on the site by itself.
5. `python3 engine/darrow.py sync`, `python3 engine/saga.py check` and `python3 engine/build_site.py` (both must pass), then `git add -A && git commit -m "checkpoint <Sunday>: chapter N <tier>" && git push origin HEAD:main`.

# /chapter: plan the next chapter

Nothing here needs the climax in memory: the brief carries what the plan must honour, so this runs as well in a fresh session as at the end of the checkpoint.

1. `git pull --rebase origin main`, then `python3 engine/saga.py now`. The previous chapter's climax must be recorded (`saga.py record` with its choice) and its week frozen (`darrow.py chapter-close`). If that climax was the Book's last (the KNOT line said the transition was due and `route decide --book N` ran at the checkpoint): `python3 engine/saga.py plan book open N+1` first (it seeds the new Book's beats and quests from the arc and folds the old Book's unwritten beats); the crossing's `### Choice` block is written when he answers, or by its default at the new chapter's first close.
2. `python3 engine/saga.py brief --chapter`: the Book's beats left and the compress rule when a Knot nears (at most two core beats a chapter: one in its week as a beat choice on a spine slot, never Saturday's, one as the climax), the shape rules, the device kinds that rest this chapter, the quests that may run (gates met, required first, never a third live), a Tether stage waiting for a float slot, the open climax choice (it carries two scenes), any approval rule the road would cross before its moment at the measured income (`saga.py approval forecast`: raise the threshold, or let the moment come sooner), the threads owed a touch with their plant counts, the arc's next beat, and the template with the device-allowed slots marked. On demand: `python3 engine/saga.py arc <id>` for a beat or a quest, `threads --id N`, `history --thread N`, `lore` (the index, once per chapter).
3. Fill the template in a scratch file: a one-line `question` (a sub-question of the Book's); 7 slots Sun–Sat with `kind` (≥ 2 `spine`, Saturday always spine, ≤ 1 `interlude`, two quest slots `float: true`), each with its `beat` or `quest`+`stage`, a one-line `plan` from the arc and a one-line `turn` (who can, must, believes, risks or chooses what by the end that they could not at the start, and what Darrow attempts); micros in non-adjacent slots touching ≥ 2 axes, each with its `default`; the **devices** by the pacing rule (one per slot, none on neighbouring days, at most two of challenge / invitation / project, one challenge, two invitations, no kind the last chapter carried): a challenge only on an Art the page has named, an invitation only from someone with a character file who is alive, where Darrow is by the page, with a `why_now` from their want and the night the spine has made, a project stage only at a place on the page and never in two chapters running; `world_moves` (three, each a thing the world did); the `climax` plan with `checks` (their DCs are advisory: the climax's brief sets them from the week's tier), 2–3 `options` and a `default` (the option the highest-approval companion present would pick; never one tagged `*[Inspiration]*`). A slot reveals a truth only when its thread shows two plants on the page; otherwise it plants. A Tether stage only in order and once its stage gate is met. The chapter touches ≥ 1 open thread and plants at most one new one. A slot's `plan` may name entries of the Annals as `lore:<id>`.
4. `python3 engine/saga.py plan chapter open --number N '<json>'` (it refuses a third live quest and an open micro, warns about empty turns, locked gates and a repeated device kind; `check` refuses the pacing and the gates). Then the chapter file `saga/chronicle/NN-<slug>.md`: `# Chapter N — Title`, the epigraph (1–3 italic lines from an in-world document, a `— source` line), `---`; a new Book opens with `# BOOK N — TITLE` above it. `python3 engine/saga.py check`, then `python3 engine/darrow.py ship -m "chapter N planned: <title>"`.
5. Afterwards a planned slot is edited with `python3 engine/saga.py plan slot N key=value …` (`--force` for a written or skipped one); a Knot tied mid-week re-plans the rest of the week toward the crossing only when the KNOT line says the transition is due at this chapter's climax.

# 24-Week Off-Season Program — v4

**For speed, cutting, and jumping sports** (ultimate, soccer, lacrosse, basketball, and similar)

**Goal:** arrive at camp fast, springy, strong, able to cut and brake at game speed, and carrying enough sport-specific running load to absorb the pre-season spike.

**Assumptions:** healthy athlete, at least one year of lifting experience, 5–6 training days available (a 4-day version is described under the weekly skeleton).

## 1. Design philosophy

Every quality is trained every week: speed, jumping, cutting, strength, and the engine. What changes from phase to phase is the emphasis and how sport-specific the work is. The pieces come from established frameworks:

- Charlie Francis's high/low sequencing
- Buchheit's 30-15 Intermittent Fitness Test (IFT) for interval prescription, with intensities set as a percentage of final test speed (VIFT)
- Harper's and Dos'Santos's deceleration and cutting research
- Velocity-loss autoregulation for strength and power work
- An eccentric → isometric → concentric strength sequence across the first three phases

**Removed from v3:** collision prep, neck work, overhead-throw bookkeeping, football position rules, and the 4-week taper.

### Phase overview

| Phase | Weeks | Emphasis | Deload |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–4 | Foundation: tissue capacity, landing and braking mechanics, aerobic base, eccentric strength | Week 4 |
| 2 | 5–8 | Build: paused/isometric strength, acceleration, planned cutting, long intervals | Week 8 |
| 3 | 9–12 | Max strength, acceleration, intensive jumps, short intervals | Week 12 (+ retest) |
| 4 | 13–16 | Power conversion: contrast lifting, max velocity, reactive plyos, reactive agility | Week 16 |
| 5 | 17–20 | Game speed and density: 1v1, small-sided games, game simulation, second-day exposure | Week 20 (light) |
| 6 | 21–24 | Peak sport-specific load (21–22), step-down (23), taper (24) | Taper |

## 2. Operating rules

1. **Speed comes first and fresh.** Sprint right after the warm-up and time it if you can. Rest about 1 minute per 10 m sprinted. End the session when times slip more than 3–5% or mechanics degrade. Get an exposure at ≥95% of max velocity in every one of the 24 weeks.
2. **Alternate high and low days.** There are three High days (linear, multidirectional, engine), each followed by a Low day. Friday's High is metabolic, not neural-max, which is why three works.
3. **Strength stays minimal and heavy.** Use 2–4 main movements per session. Stop strength sets at about 20% velocity loss or 2 reps in reserve. Stop power sets at about 10% velocity loss.
4. **Count plyos in contacts and climb a ladder:** landing → extensive (submax) → intensive (max-effort jumps) → reactive (ground contact under 0.25 s). Cover vertical, horizontal, and lateral directions, on one leg and two. Leave 48–72 h between intensive or reactive sessions.
5. **Cutting has four stages:** braking mechanics → planned cuts (45°/90°/180°) → reactive (mirror, cue) → game speed (1v1, small-sided). Do equal reps on both sides, rest 45–90 s between reps, and stop when quality drops.
6. **Tissue work runs year-round at low doses:** Nordics, Copenhagens, heavy calf work, Achilles and patellar tendon isometrics, ankle balance and peroneals, tibialis, and trunk.
7. **Change one volume dial per week.** Never raise sprint meters, plyo contacts, and conditioning volume in the same week. Run 3 weeks up and 1 week down (deload = volume −30–40%, intensity held).
8. **Arrive at camp loaded.** The highest sport-specific running volume comes in Weeks 21–22, followed by a 7–10 day taper.

## 3. Weekly skeleton

This structure stays constant across all 24 weeks. The phases change what fills each slot.

| Day | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **HIGH** (linear) | Sprint → vertical/horizontal plyos → rotational throws → Lower A (bilateral + hinge) → Nordics |
| Tue | LOW | Extensive tempo → Upper A → trunk → tendon/calf block |
| Wed | **HIGH** (multidirectional) | Decel/cutting/agility → lateral and single-leg plyos → throws → Lower B (unilateral + frontal plane) → Copenhagens |
| Thu | LOW | Zone 2 (off-feet if legs are beat) + mobility + ankle/shoulder robustness |
| Fri | **HIGH** (engine) | 2–3 short sprints → main conditioning → Upper B → tendon/calf block |
| Sat | LOW / optional | Sport skill + easy aerobic; becomes a second game-simulation day in Phases 5–6 |
| Sun | OFF | |

**Four-day version:** keep Mon/Wed/Fri as written and fold Tue and Thu into a single Low day. If time gets short, cut Low-day volume and keep the speed work.

**Tendon/calf block (Tue and Fri):** seated calf raise, single-leg standing calf raise, Achilles isometric holds (straight and bent knee), Spanish-squat isometric hold, tibialis raises, single-leg balance. Doses are listed in each phase.

**Ankle/shoulder robustness (Thu):** single-leg balance with perturbation, banded eversion for the peroneals, multi-directional hop-and-stick, Y/T/W raises, external rotation, push-up plus.

---

## 4. Warm-up and footwear

**Warm-up (every field day, 10–15 min):** FIFA 11+ style neuromuscular warm-up → hip, ankle, and thoracic-spine mobility → adductor rockbacks 1–2×8/side → 2–3 build-up runs.

**Footwear and surface progression:** trainers → flats/turf shoes → cleats or spikes. Move up only when tissues are quiet (no hot spots, no Achilles reactivity). Step back one level if Achilles or shin soreness is over 2/10 the next day.

## 5. Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Deload: Week 4.** Strength emphasis: slow eccentrics.

### Speed (Mon)
- 6×10 m accelerations from falling or half-kneeling starts
- 4×30 m build-ups
- Add 3× flying 10 m in Week 2, 4× in Week 3

### Plyos
- Landing work: snap-downs, single-leg drop-and-stick from 20–30 cm
- Extensive work: pogos, line hops, skips, low box jumps
- 80–120 submax contacts per session

### Cutting — stage 1, braking mechanics (Wed)
- Decelerate from 10 m, then 15 m, then 20 m to a split-stance stick
- Lateral shuffle-to-stick
- 8–10 total reps; entry speed rises from 60–70% to 85% across the phase

### Lower A (Mon)
- Trap-bar deadlift or squat 3×6 @ 65–75%, 4 s lowering
- RDL 3×8
- Nordics: Week 1 2×3 → Week 2 2×4 → Week 3 3×4

### Lower B (Wed)
- Rear-foot-elevated split squat 3×6/side, 3 s lowering
- Lateral lunge 3×6/side
- Single-leg RDL 3×8/side
- Short-lever Copenhagen 2×20 s/side

### Upper (Tue, Fri)
- One push, two pulls, one landmine or overhead movement; all 3×8–10

### Rotational
- Anti-rotation first: Pallof press, side plank, suitcase carry
- Technique throws with 1–2 kg: scoop toss and shot-put throw, 3×5/side each

### Engine
- Tue: 8–12×100 m @ ~70% speed, walk back
- Thu: Zone 2, 30–40 min
- Fri: 3×4 min building to 4×4 min @ RPE 7–8, 3 min easy between

### Tissue block
- Seated calf raise 3×10–12
- Single-leg standing calf raise 3×8
- Achilles isometric 3×30 s
- Spanish-squat isometric 3×30 s
- Tibialis raises 2×15
- Single-leg balance 2×30 s/side

## 6. Phase 2 — Build (Weeks 5–8)

**Deload: Week 8.** Strength emphasis: pauses and isometrics.

### Speed (Mon)
- 6–8×20 m accelerations, including 4×15 m heavy sled (loaded to cut your speed roughly in half)
- 4× flying 10–15 m
- About 250–300 m total

### Plyos
- Box jumps, broad jumps, and countermovement jumps 4×4
- Submax single-leg hops
- Lateral bound-and-stick 3×4/side
- 60–80 contacts, no more than 30 at max effort

### Cutting — stage 2, planned cuts (Wed)
- 45° cuts and 180° turns, 5–6 per side
- Approach speed rises from 75% to 90%
- Cues: brake on the second-to-last step, keep the hips low, plant outside your base

### Lower A (Mon)
- Squat or trap bar 4×5 @ 75–82% with a 2–3 s pause at the bottom
- RDL 3×6
- Nordics 3×4–5

### Lower B (Wed)
- Paused split squat 4×5/side
- Cossack or lateral lunge 3×6/side
- Single-leg RDL 3×6/side
- Long-lever Copenhagen 3×20–25 s/side

### Upper
- Main push and pull 4×6–8; accessories 3×8–10

### Rotational
- 2–3 kg balls
- Add step-behind shot-put throw, rotational slam, landmine rotation 3×6/side

### Engine
- Tue: tempo 1200–1600 m total (12–16×100 m or 6–8×200 m)
- Thu: Zone 2, 40–45 min
- Fri: 4×4 min @ 90–95% HRmax, building to 5×4 min by Week 7

### Tissue block
- Isometrics progress to 4×40–45 s heavy, but only if next-day tendon pain is ≤2/10
- Calf work heavier: seated 4×8, single-leg standing 3×6–8 loaded

## 7. Phase 3 — Max strength and acceleration (Weeks 9–12)

**Deload and retest: Week 12.** Strength emphasis: heavy concentric.

### Speed (Mon)
- 6×30 m accelerations (2–3 with a light sled)
- 3–4× flying 20 m off a 30 m build
- About 300–350 m total

### Plyos
- Approach jumps off the left leg, right leg, and two feet, 4×3 each
- Broad jump into a vertical jump 3×3
- Low hurdle hops 4×5
- Continuous lateral bounds 3×6
- 50–60 contacts

### Cutting — stage 2 → 3 (Wed)
- 90° and 180° cuts at full approach speed
- Curved sprints 4×30 m
- Partner mirror drill 6×6–8 s

### Lower A (Mon)
- Main lift 5×3 or 4×4 @ 82–90%
- Trap-bar jump 4×3 @ 20–30%
- Nordics 2×5–6 (a maintenance dose is enough from here)

### Lower B (Wed)
- Heavy split squat or step-up 4×4/side
- Heavy lateral lunge 3×5/side
- Single-leg hip thrust or 45° back extension 3×6–8
- Copenhagen reps 3×6–8/side

### Upper
- Main push and pull 4×3–5; accessories 3×6–8

### Rotational
- 3–4 kg strength-speed throws plus 1–2 kg max-velocity throws
- Heavy cable or landmine rotation 3×5/side

### Engine
- Tue: tempo ~2000 m
- Thu: Zone 2, 40–45 min
- Fri: 30 s on/30 s off @ ~90% VIFT for 2×8 min, progressing to 15 s/15 s @ ~95% VIFT for 3×7 min by Week 11
- No 30-15 IFT result? Use RPE 8–9 and finish each set feeling you could manage 1–2 more reps.

## 8. Phase 4 — Power conversion (Weeks 13–16)

**Deload: Week 16.**

### Speed (Mon) — max-velocity emphasis
- Wicket runs (sprinting over evenly spaced mini-hurdles) 3–4 runs
- 4–5× flying 20–30 m
- Sprint-float-sprint 2×60 m
- 4×20 m accelerations
- About 350–400 m total with full rest

### Plyos — reactive
- Drop jumps 4×4 from whichever height gives your best reactive strength index (usually 30–45 cm)
- Hurdle hops 4×5
- Alternate-leg and single-leg bounds 4×20 m
- Depth drop into an approach jump 3×3
- 40–60 contacts

### Agility — stage 3, reactive (Wed)
- Cuts off a visual or partner cue
- 1v1 evasion in a 10×10 m box
- 8–12 reps of 5–8 s with full recovery
- Add the ball or disc

### Lower A (Mon) — French contrast, 3–4 rounds
1. Squat or trap bar ×2–3 @ 85–90%
2. Hurdle hops ×4
3. Trap-bar jump ×3 @ 30%
4. Band-assisted jump ×4

Rest 20–30 s between exercises and 3–4 min between rounds.

### Lower B (Wed)
- Split squat 3×3–4/side paired with single-leg or lateral bounds ×3/side
- Hinge 3×4
- Copenhagens maintained 2×6–8/side

### Upper
- Push press or landmine punch paired with plyo push-ups
- Heavy pull 4×3–5

### Rotational
- Max intent with 1–3 kg, 20–30 throws per session
- Moving starts: shuffle-in, step-behind
- Catch-and-stick for rotational deceleration

### Engine — alternate these two Friday sessions weekly
- Repeated sprints: 2–3×(6×30 m, 20 s rest), 4 min between sets
- Shuttle 15 s/15 s @ 95% VIFT with 180° turns, 2–3×6 min

Tue tempo ~1600–2000 m; Thu Zone 2 30–40 min.

## 9. Phase 5 — Game speed and density (Weeks 17–20)

**Light deload: Week 20 (volume −25–30%).**

### Speed (Mon)
- Sprint-to-jump
- Sprint-to-cut
- Curved flying 20s
- 3–4 timed flying 20–30 m
- About 250–300 m total (game simulation now adds high-speed running)

### Plyos
- Full run-up approach jumps reading a tossed ball or disc
- Contested jumps
- Drop jumps 3×3
- 30–40 contacts, all high quality

### Agility — stage 4, game speed (Wed)
- 1v1, 2v2, and small-sided games
- Play both offense and defense roles

### Strength — two 30–40 minute sessions (Mon, Wed)
- Main lift 3×2–3 @ 85–88%
- Loaded jump 3×3 @ 30–40%
- One unilateral lift 2–3×4/side
- Nordics 2×4
- Copenhagens 2×/week

### Rotational
- 1–3 kg, 16–24 max-intent throws, sport-pattern footwork

### Engine
- Fri — game simulation, either of:
  - 2–3 sets of 6×45–60 s of sport-pattern work (cuts, jumps, re-accelerations) with 60–90 s rest and 4 min between sets
  - 4×4 min small-sided games
- Sat — second game-simulation day from Week 18, at 50% of Friday's volume, rising to 80% by Week 19
- Tue tempo 1200–1600 m; Thu Zone 2 30 min

## 10. Phase 6 — Peak specific load, then taper (Weeks 21–24)

### Weeks 21–22 — highest sport-specific running load of the plan
- Fri and Sat: back-to-back game simulation or scrimmages at full volume
- Mon: low-volume timed speed (3–4 flying sprints, a few accelerations)
- Wed: agility plus a short lift
- Week 22: the 30-15 IFT opens Friday's session

### Week 23 — step-down
- Volume −25–30%, intensity held
- The last hard game simulation falls 8–10 days before camp

### Week 24 — taper
- Volume −50–60%, same frequency, same intensity
- Mon: timed sprints and jumps (this doubles as your retest)
- Wed: 2×2 heavy plus 3×3 jumps
- Thu or Fri: one game simulation at 40–50% volume
- Tissue work at one light set each
- Two easy days before camp

## 11. Testing and readiness

### Full battery — Weeks 0, 12, and 24

| Quality | Test |
|---|---|
| Speed | 10 m and 30 m sprint with a flying-10 split |
| Vertical power | Countermovement jump |
| Sport jump | Approach-jump touch height: left leg, right leg, two feet |
| Reactive strength | 10-5 repeated-jump test or drop-jump reactive strength index |
| Asymmetry | Single-leg hop or single-leg jump, left vs right (flag anything over 10–15%) |
| Change of direction | 505 on both sides, plus COD deficit (505 time minus 10 m time) |
| Strength | Trap-bar 3RM or isometric mid-thigh pull |
| Rotational power | Rotational med-ball throw, both sides |
| Groin | Copenhagen hold time |
| Calf | Single-leg calf-raise reps to tempo |

### Aerobic — Weeks 0, 12, and 22
- 30-15 IFT (sets VIFT for interval prescription)

### Weekly tracking
- Best flying-10 time
- Monday jump score (countermovement jump or reactive strength index)
- Session RPE × minutes
- Wellness score 1–5
- Sprint meters at ≥95%
- Plyo contacts
- Body mass (same day and time)

### Readiness rule
If the flying-10 time is 3–5% slow or the Monday jump is 10% down, cut that day's volume 30–50% and keep intensity. If that happens two Mondays in a row, deload early.

### Pain rule
If tendon or joint pain is over 3/10 during a session or over 2/10 the next morning, step back one rung on that ladder. If it lasts over 48 hours, swap impact work for bike or sled.

### Tissue thresholds
- Copenhagen hold under 45 s, or groin strength down more than 10%: add one Copenhagen set that week and trim cutting volume 10–20%.
- Single-leg calf raise under 25 reps/leg: add one soleus set and reduce bounds 20% for 7 days.

## 12. Sport dials

### Ultimate
- Use the Thursday robustness slot for layout prep: push-up plus, crawls, plyo push-ups, shoulder rolls, then low dives on a soft surface.
- Add disc throwing on Low days and build weekly volume gradually, because the forehand loads the elbow and wrist.
- Bias jumps toward single-leg approach jumps with a disc read.
- Keep the Saturday second-day sessions. They are your tournament preparation.

### Court sports (basketball, volleyball, handball)
- If you're playing pickup in the off-season, cut programmed plyo contacts 30–40%.
- Prioritize the patellar tendon isometrics.
- Shorten flying sprints to 10–15 m and spend more of the speed budget on acceleration.

### Big-field sports (soccer, lacrosse, field hockey)
- Lengthen the flying sprints to 30–40 m.
- Push tempo and high-speed running volume about 20% higher.
- Use sport-specific small-sided games for the Friday engine session from Phase 4 onward.

## 13. Recovery and fueling

- **Sleep:** 8–9 h per night.
- **Protein:** 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day across 3–5 feedings.
- **Carbohydrate:** 5–8 g/kg/day on heavy field days. Add 1–2 g/kg the day before key speed, contrast, or game-simulation days in Phases 4–6. During long or intense sessions, take 30–60 g/h with fluids and electrolytes.
- **Body mass:** weigh in weekly, same day and time. Avoid large deficits from Phase 4 onward.

## 14. Glossary

- **Flying sprint:** a sprint timed over a set distance after a run-in, so the timed zone is at or near top speed.
- **VIFT:** final speed reached in the 30-15 Intermittent Fitness Test, used to set interval running speeds.
- **Velocity loss:** the percentage drop in bar speed from the fastest rep in a set; used as a stop signal.
- **Reactive strength index (RSI):** jump height divided by ground contact time.
- **COD deficit:** 505 time minus 10 m sprint time; isolates turning ability from straight-line speed.
- **French contrast:** a four-exercise power circuit: heavy lift, bodyweight plyo, loaded jump, assisted jump.
- **Extensive tempo:** submax runs (~65–75% speed) with walk-back recovery, used for aerobic work without pounding.
- **Copenhagen:** side plank with the top leg supported on a bench, loading the adductors.
- **Spanish squat:** squat hold with a band behind the knees, loading the patellar tendon.
- **Game simulation:** conditioning built from sport-pattern work (cuts, jumps, re-accelerations) at match work-to-rest ratios.

# Body Measurement Guide

*His guide for the tape girths, as he followed it. First set: Wed 2026-08-12, first thing in the morning. Reference: never edited here; a new version replaces it whole. Sites → `config.json → measurement_metrics`; the numbers → `measurements.csv` (`python3 engine/darrow.py measures`).*

## Sites

- **Waist:** Measure around the midpoint between the lowest rib and top of the hip bone. Stand relaxed and measure after a normal exhale. Do not suck in or flex.
- **Chest:** Measure around the chest at nipple level. Stand naturally with arms relaxed and chest unflexed.
- **Shoulders:** Measure the full circumference around the widest part of the shoulders/delts and upper chest.
- **Upper Arm – Relaxed:** Measure around the widest part of the upper arm with the arm hanging naturally and muscles relaxed.
- **Upper Arm – Flexed:** Measure around the widest part of the upper arm while flexing the biceps, without excessively squeezing the tape.
- **Forearm:** Measure around the widest part of the forearm with the arm relaxed.
- **Neck:** Measure around the neck just below the Adam’s apple, keeping the tape level and the neck relaxed.
- **Thigh / Quad:** Measure around the widest part of the upper thigh while standing relaxed with weight distributed evenly.
- **Calf:** Measure around the widest part of the calf while standing normally with the calf relaxed.
- **Hips / Glutes:** Measure around the widest part of the hips and buttocks, keeping the tape level.
- **Wrist:** Measure around the narrowest part of the wrist, just above the wrist bones.
- **Ankle:** Measure around the narrowest part of the ankle, just above the ankle bones.

## Conditions

For consistency, take all measurements under the same conditions each time, ideally in the morning before training, with the tape snug against the skin but not compressing it.

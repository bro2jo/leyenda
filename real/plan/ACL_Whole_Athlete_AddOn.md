## 2. Operating rules

1. **Daily minimum first.** The morning check and the weighted heel prop are done every day before any add-on work. A week with a missed daily-minimum day costs the following week its optional items (accessory day, Saturday session).
2. **Left leg up between upper sets.** Heel on a bench, knee unsupported, quad relaxed. Rest periods are extra heel-prop minutes.
3. **Seated or lying for the first two upper sessions** (re-entry). None has run yet; the first is planned Fri 10/9.
   - From the third session: pull-ups with a box (step on and off with hands on the bar; a drop is a landing), half-kneeling with the right knee down, standing landmine press, standing Pallof.
   - No kneeling of any kind on the left, which is the harvest site. On the chest-supported row, feet stay on the floor; don't kneel on the seat.
4. **Gym minutes on feet are logged.** Loaded carries wait for Phase 4 (section 10).
5. **Upper body is not a knee variable.** Calibration runs were dropped 10/4. Three weeks of evidence say seated lifting with the leg up won't show in the morning: 5-, 7- and ~12-change knee batches, a back-to-back loaded pair, and hours on feet all produced clean mornings. If a morning after a knee day isn't clean, reverse the knee batch first. Check the upper setup (leg hanging, walking around) before cutting any lifting.
6. **Upper body and right leg progress on their own criteria** (section 6). A heavier bench is not a knee variable.
7. **Bad mornings:**
   - **Pre-check fails (yellow):** the knee session shrinks per the Working Rules; upper body runs as written.
   - **Plan red light** (new swelling, extension loss, giving way): contact the PT first. Seated upper work is still available after that.
   - **Post-op red flags** (fever, calf pain or swelling, chest pain, shortness of breath, wound changes): no training of any kind; call the surgical team.
8. **Three weeks up, one down** on upper volume. Deload = sets −30–40%, loads held.
9. **Short on time — cut in this order:** the C pair of the upper core → the whole upper core → the accessory day. Never the daily minimum, never the knee session's minimum.

**A block advances** only if upper ran 2 of 2 in at least two of its weeks; otherwise it repeats.

**Load goes up when:** all reps are clean, bar speed is steady, 2+ reps remain in reserve, and nothing about the setup bothered the knee. Then: +5 lb on the barbell, the smallest DB step, or one band step on pulls. With a velocity device: stop strength sets at ~20% velocity loss, power sets at ~10%.

**Posture and neck work** progresses by hold time and position quality, never load.

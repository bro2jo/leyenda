## Daily Targets
 
### Calories
 
Start: ~3,000 kcal/day
 
After 2 consistent weeks:
 
- Still losing/flat → add 150–250 kcal/day
- Gaining ~0.25–0.75 lb/week → stay
- Consistently >1 lb/week after initial rebound → reduce ~150–200 kcal
### Protein
 
150–160 g/day
 
Aim for ~4 feedings:
 
- Breakfast: 30–40 g
- Lunch: 35–45 g
- Snack/shake: 30–40 g
- Dinner/evening: 35–45 g
Good sources: eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, milk, chicken, beef, turkey, fish, whey
 
### Carbohydrates
 
~350–400 g/day
 
Increase as running/cardio/training volume rises.
 
Main sources: rice, potatoes, oats, pasta, bread, cereal, fruit, milk/yogurt, beans, tortillas
 
### Fat
 
~85–100 g/day
 
Useful calorie-dense sources: olive oil, nut butter, nuts, avocado, whole eggs, cheese, salmon, full/2% dairy

## Meal Formula
 
For lunch/dinner: **Protein + large carb + produce + calorie booster**
 
Examples:
 
- Chicken + rice + vegetables + olive oil
- Beef + pasta + sauce + parmesan
- Salmon + potatoes + vegetables + olive oil
Use olive oil, nut butter, dairy, etc. to increase calories without huge food volume.

## Supplements
 
### Tier 1: Creatine
 
3–5 g creatine monohydrate daily
 
Purpose: strength, muscle gain, whole-body performance
 
Not proven to accelerate ACL healing. No cycling or loading needed.
 
### Whey Protein
 
Use only as needed to reach 150–160 g protein/day. Typical serving: 25–35 g protein.
 
### Collagen / Gelatin
 
Optional. Because of patellar-tendon graft:
 
- 10–15 g collagen or gelatin, 30–60 min before knee-loading/PT
- Add ~50 mg vitamin C from food or supplement
Potentially useful for collagen remodeling, but not proven to accelerate ACL graft healing. Do not use collagen instead of whey/meat/dairy for muscle protein.
 
### Vitamin D
 
Test, don't guess. Ask PCP/surgeon for 25-hydroxy vitamin D. Supplement if deficient based on medical guidance.
 
### Omega-3
 
Preferred: fatty fish 2–3x/week. Fish-oil supplement: optional, not essential.
 
### Skip
 
Usually unnecessary if protein intake is adequate: BCAAs, glutamine, EAA drinks, testosterone boosters, expensive recovery blends, mass gainers, megadose vitamins.

## Food Quality
 
Most days include:
 
- 2–4 servings fruit
- Several servings vegetables
- Calcium-rich foods
- Whole grains / rice / potatoes / oats
- Nuts/seeds/olive oil
- Meat/fish/eggs/dairy/legumes
- ~25–35 g fiber/day
Do not eat so much low-calorie/high-fiber food that appetite becomes worse.

## Micronutrient Reference (added Oct 8, 2026)
 
Standard adult-male reference values (DRI / FDA Daily Value). These are not targets from the original guide and do not change the calorie/protein priority. Use them to judge food quality week to week, not as daily must-hits.
 
| Nutrient | Daily reference | Why it matters here | Main sources in current diet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturated fat | <~33 g (10% of 3,000 kcal) | Heart health; cheese/fried food overshoot it fast | Cheddar, butter, fried food |
| Sugar | <~75 g (10% of kcal) | Not a hard limit while regaining; mostly drink/sauce calories | Lemonade, BBQ/CFA sauce, candy |
| Sodium | <2,300 mg (up to ~3,000 ok on heavy-sweat days) | Fluid balance; restaurant days land 3,000–6,000 | Takeout, deli meat, sauces, breaded tenders |
| Potassium | 3,400 mg | Muscle/nerve function, offsets sodium | Potatoes, fruit, beans, avocado, dairy |
| Calcium | 1,000 mg | Bone and graft site | Dairy, Premier shake, Oikos |
| Iron | 8 mg | Oxygen transport, recovery | Beef, chicken, beans, enriched rice |
| Magnesium | 420 mg | Muscle function, sleep | Nuts, beans, oats, brown rice, avocado |
| Zinc | 11 mg | Tissue repair, immune | Beef, chicken, dairy, fortified shake |
| Vitamin C | 90 mg | Collagen synthesis (graft) | Fruit, peppers, broccoli, potatoes |
| Vitamin D | 15–20 mcg (600–800 IU) | Bone, muscle; test per Supplements | Fortified dairy/shakes, fatty fish, sun |
| Omega-3 (EPA+DHA) | ~250–500 mg | Anti-inflammatory; fish 2–3x/wk per Supplements | Salmon, sardines, tuna; fish oil optional |
| Fiber | 25–35 g | Already above; don't overshoot if it kills appetite | Fruit, beans, oats, vegetables |

## Hydration
 
- Drink consistently
- Pale-yellow urine is a reasonable target
- Normal salt intake unless medically restricted
- Increase fluids/sodium later as cardio volume rises
---
 
## Alcohol
 
Keep low. Frequent/heavy alcohol can hurt sleep, recovery, food intake, and muscle adaptation.

## As Training Returns
 
### Early rehab
 
- ~3,000 kcal
- 150–160 g protein
- Daily shake
- Creatine
- Structured meals
### Strength phase
 
- Maintain small calorie surplus
- Increase carbs as workload rises
- Regain 160–164 lb
### Running/cardio phase
 
- Increase calories with activity
- Most extra calories can come from carbs
### Lean gain phase
 
- Slowly progress toward ~170 lb
- Prioritize performance and body composition over exact scale weight

## Weekly Scorecard
 
Track only:
 
- Average morning bodyweight
- Average calories
- Protein ≥150 g/day
- Daily shake/meals completed
- Rehab/training progression
- Micro flags: any nutrient below reference most days; sodium / sat fat / sugar well above reference most days; fish 0x that week
Weigh 3–4+ mornings/week: after bathroom, before food/drink, similar conditions. Use the weekly average, not individual days.

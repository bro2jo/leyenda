# Art — pictures and songs for the site

Images and songs the user supplies for the Chronicle's public site (`docs/`). Reader-safe only: a picture may show only what the page has already put on the page, and a song may sing only what the page has already sung.

- `places/<place-id>.webp` (or `.png`, `.jpg`): one picture per place, named for its id in `saga/state/places.json`.
- `factions/<faction-id>.jpg` (or `.webp`, `.png`): one picture per faction, named for its id in `saga/state/factions.json`, set the same way as a place (`image`, `image_alt`, `image_caption`, `image_focus`) and shown in its Codex entry the same way.
- `codex/<slug>.jpg` (or `.webp`, `.png`): a picture for a codex.md entry (a thing or a beast, e.g. `codex/thistle.jpg`), set in `saga/state/codex_art.json` as `{"name": "<the entry's bold name exactly>", "image", "image_alt", "image_caption", "image_focus"}` and shown in its Codex entry, and on the link preview of its name in the story, the same way as a place. Not for Sayings, and not for a person, place or faction that has its own file (its picture goes there); the build refuses either.
- `songs/<slug>.mp3` (or `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.opus`; up to 10 MB): a song for a codex.md entry under **Songs and verses** (e.g. `songs/count-the-lances.mp3` for *The Lowmarch counting rhyme*), set in `saga/state/codex_art.json` as `{"name": "<the entry's bold name exactly>", "audio": "songs/<slug>.mp3", "audio_title": "<the title its player shows>"}` (an entry may carry a picture too). The Codex entry gets a player and a ♪ in its row; in the story a small play button follows the first linked mention of the song's name in a scene, or ends the first paragraph, quote or epigraph that sings a line its codex.md entry quotes in italics. One song plays at a time; while it plays out of sight a small dock keeps a pause button at the bottom of the screen. The build reads an MP3's embedded lyrics and refuses the file if any line is not already in the chronicle (a stanza the page has not sung stays in the Annals); a file without embedded lyrics can't be checked, so listen before filing it.
- `arts/<art-id>.webp` (or `.png`, `.jpg`): a card picture for one of Darrow's Arts, named for its `id` in `engine/rules.json` (`arts`, and `sport_arts.arts`), e.g. `arts/stillwater.webp`. Nothing else to set: the build finds it by name and shows it on Darrow's page in the Art's row, framed in the metal of its rank (iron, brass, steel, silver, gold for I–V), greyed while the Art is sealed, and hidden until the Art has its first rank (a dashed blank stands there before). Square works best; it shows at 64 px and opens whole when tapped. A file named for no Art fails the build. Draw the technique, never a person or place the page has not shown.
- `figure/<rank>.webp` (or `.png`, `.jpg`): Darrow's full-length figure at one rank of the level ladder, named for the rank in lower case with hyphens: `bound`, `kindled`, `tempered`, `warden-errant`, `emberknight`, `unbowed`, `warden-of-the-ember`. Nothing else to set: the build shows the figures of every rank he has reached at the head of his Reckoning on his page, the current rank first, in a tall 3:4 frame of his rank's metal that swipes back through the earlier ones (a tab per rank). A rank not yet reached stays hidden, so a figure can be drawn ahead. Draw the same man each time and change only his build and bearing (fuller through the chest and shoulders, standing straighter, his weight on both legs): what he wears and carries belongs to the story's portraits. A file named for no rank fails the build.
- `characters/<character-id>.jpg` (or `.webp`, `.png`): one portrait per character, named for their file in `saga/characters/`. Square works best.
- To publish a place picture, set the place's `image` (path under `saga/art/`, e.g. `places/saint-ysoldes.webp`), `image_alt` (what the picture shows) and optionally `image_caption`. The place must already be `on_page`.
- `python3 engine/build_site.py` copies it to `docs/art/…` and shows it in the place's Codex entry: full width, a thumbnail in the row, tap or click to see it whole. Keep each file under 3 MB (the build refuses larger); WebP around 1600 px wide is plenty.
- To publish a portrait, set `portrait` (path under `saga/art/`, e.g. `characters/darrow.jpg`) and `portrait_alt` in the character's file. The site shows it framed at the head of their page (tap to enlarge), round on their roster card, and, for Darrow, on the Now page's Reckoning card. A portrait shows only what the page allows: a character not yet seen has no portrait.
- **A new portrait for a new Book** (a changed look: the road instead of the House) goes in as `characters/<character-id>-bookN.jpg`; the old file stays where it is. In the character's file the new one becomes `portrait`/`portrait_alt` with `portrait_label` (e.g. `"Book II"`), and the old one moves, with its alt text and focus, into `portraits_earlier` as `{"label": "Book I", "portrait", "portrait_alt", "portrait_focus"}`, newest first. The character page's frame then swipes between them, with a tab for each under it; avatars and previews show only the current one. The new picture still shows only what the page allows. A wider shot, where the face is small, takes `portrait_zoom` (1–4): the avatars and name previews zoom in on `portrait_focus` while the page's frame shows the whole picture.
- **Sizing is automatic.** Every picture sits in a fixed frame and is cropped to fit, never stretched: place pictures 16:9 at the full width of the column, portraits a square that scales with the screen (about a third of a phone's width, 200 px on anything wider) with the level crest on its corner. The crest, the frame and the plate's rivets take the metal of the character's rank: iron, brass, steel, silver, ember, gold, white-gold (`METALS` in `engine/build_site.py`, one per rank of `engine/rules.json → levels.ranks`). Any shape or size of upload works; tapping shows the whole uncropped picture. If the crop cuts off something that matters, set `image_focus` or `portrait_focus` to the point to keep centred, as `"X% Y%"` (e.g. `"50% 20%"` keeps the top of a tall portrait).
- **Small copies are automatic too.** `python3 engine/thumbs.py` (which `darrow.py ship` runs before the build) makes a small (256 px) and a medium (640 px) WebP copy of every picture here into `thumbs/`, with `thumbs/manifest.json` naming the file each was made from; never edit `thumbs/` by hand. The site shows the copies wherever a picture is small (avatars, Codex rows, name previews, a portrait's frame) and the full picture only when it is opened: a Codex entry unfolded, a picture tapped to see it whole. A picture added or replaced without fresh copies still shows, at full size, and the build prints a note naming it. Needs Pillow.
- `site/`: the favicon's bitmaps (`favicon-32.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` for a phone's home screen), the same drawing as `FAVICON` in `engine/build_site.py`; redraw both if that changes.

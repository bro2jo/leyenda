# NOW — the Chronicle

*Narrative only. Nothing from the real logs appears on this page except through the Reckoning.*

**BOOK I — THE HOUSE OF MENDERS · Chapter 2 — The Grey Cot · the day after the feast**

- **Where:** Saint Ysolde's, the House of Menders, high in the Greywater; the infirmary hall in the evening of the day after the feast, halfway back up the row from the kitchen door, the kitchen's book open behind him. Below the gate the Confessors' fire burns on the lower terrace.
- **The quest:** *The List.* Confessor Ivo Marrant read his writ at the bars: Darrow first, Hollis second, the far cots not read ("The Crown has no quarrel with men who can't answer"). Two grey cloaks came through the wicket before Old Mercy rang, late; the Prior spoke the law of names, and they went back out. Marrant camped on the lower terrace ("Bells grow tired") and called for the Captain of the Ninth to come down and kneel, and the rest of the paper would go back in his cloak. Darrow went to the bars and gave him his name and his face. Marrant says he will wait.
- **What Darrow is fighting right now:** the soft season, and the waiting. The knee is held, and still soft. Benedek lies Hollowed in the Grey Cot, nobody passed in the night, and the Confessor's word for it has come up the Steps. Under it is the thing he has said only to Maelis: a lad who can't answer has no will for the bell to keep.
- **Just happened (Between):** *The Book.* The kitchen's book had reopened, taking odds on which cot is Hollowed next: Hollis at four, the Captain of the Ninth at five (*"Because the Confessor came up the Steps for you"*), the Grey Cot with no price because nothing is left there to take. The novice who runs it: *"If it's in the book it's a number, Ser, and you can rub a number out."* Darrow didn't shut it. He had her write NOBODY at the foot of the column and staked a chore: another cot grey before the thaw and he scours the ovens; none, and every price is rubbed out. They shook on it. Halfway back up the hall, a lay brother was asking the price on nobody.
- **Earlier today, in order:** *Nobody Passed* (Scene 3): the far cots' grey blanket brought to the lad, and the hall calls it the Grey Cot; the Confessor's word came up the Steps, *ember witchcraft*; Darrow read the night novice and the kitchen and held Wren to her count, then told the hall nobody passed, not the Mender either; Hollis, to the lad's hand: *"Nobody."*
- **With him:** Hollis Garrow (second on the list; dry, and has told Darrow so, nobody else; sworn to say his thoughts aloud; sits the watch at the Grey Cot and would not let the lad be moved; ate only when Darrow held the lad's hand for him, and Darrow has told nobody how) · Ser Benedek Orrin (Hollowed: breathing, eyes open; in the Grey Cot, under the far cots' grey blanket) · Maelis Vorne (read the knee: held, and still soft; found the black glass, and has it in her coat; will say only that it is not the far cots' sickness; has ordered him the slow way, every time, and sent him to make Hollis eat) · Wren Ashdown (his legs; counts his steps for the Mender at every bell; will not look at the Grey Cot; has his promise to tell her first, in her ledger) · Mother Ione (would not reproach him; he is in the names she says every night) · Rae Thorne (her shoulder shut the wicket where his could not; her cart still under a grey cloak's boots) · three men of the Ninth, read with him at the gate, near the fire at the gate end (they will not go down the Steps on his back; he has promised they will be asked; the oldest was awake all night).
- **Also in the House:** Sister Pell, keeper of the bells, whose hands were slow on the rope · the far cots, which were not read.
- **Against him:** Confessor Ivo Marrant and four grey cloaks sitting up in turns on the lower terrace, who have his name and his face, and who have named Benedek's Hollowing *ember witchcraft*; the sixth at the winch-house.
- **Next:** the questions the House is asking now that nobody passed; and the road down the Steps, which the Confessors still hold.
- **Open choice:** none. The next comes at Chapter 2's climax.

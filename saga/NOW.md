# NOW — the Chronicle

*Narrative only. Nothing from the real logs appears on this page except through the Reckoning.*

**BOOK I — THE HOUSE OF MENDERS · Chapter 2 — The Grey Cot · the feast morning**

- **Where:** Saint Ysolde's, the House of Menders, high in the Greywater; the infirmary hall on the feast morning, sitting on the end of his cot by Hollis's, who holds Benedek's hand across the gap. The kitchen is counting loaves. Below the gate the Confessors' fire has been let burn up.
- **The quest:** *The List.* Confessor Ivo Marrant read his writ at the bars: Darrow first, Hollis second, the far cots not read ("The Crown has no quarrel with men who can't answer"). Two grey cloaks came through the wicket before Old Mercy rang, late; the Prior spoke the law of names, and they went back out. Marrant camped on the lower terrace ("Bells grow tired") and called for the Captain of the Ninth to come down and kneel, and the rest of the paper would go back in his cloak. Darrow went to the bars and gave him his name and his face. Marrant says he will wait.
- **What Darrow is fighting right now:** the soft season, and the waiting. Maelis read the knee by lamplight after the wicket: *"Held."* It is still soft, and will be at the thaw. Now a lad of the Sixth lies Hollowed two cots away, and nobody came up the row in the night.
- **Just happened (Scene 2):** *Feast Bread.* The lay brothers carried the feast bread down to the gate under the grey cloaks' eyes while the kitchen ran a book on whether they'd take it. At the dawn bell Benedek's lips did not move: breathing, eyes open on the roof, frost on his mouth. The hall said *Hollowed* and turned to the door; Darrow held it on the lad: *"Not the door. Here. Look at his mouth."* Maelis found a chip of black glass under his tongue, *"The far cots never had this,"* and said nothing else. Hollis took the lad's hand in both of his and said it out loud, as he had sworn: *"I told him to get some sleep."* The kitchen began counting loaves. The oldest of Darrow's three, awake by the fire all night: *"Nobody passed, Captain."*
- **Earlier today, in order:** nothing yet.
- **With him:** Hollis Garrow (second on the list; dry, and has told Darrow so, nobody else; sworn to say his thoughts aloud; holding Benedek's hand and will not let go) · Ser Benedek Orrin (Hollowed in the night: breathing, eyes open, frost on his mouth) · Maelis Vorne (read the knee: held, and still soft; found the black glass, and has it in her coat) · Wren Ashdown (ran for the bell on his word; has his promise to tell her first, in her ledger) · Mother Ione (would not reproach him; he is in the names she says every night) · Rae Thorne (her shoulder shut the wicket where his could not; her cart still under a grey cloak's boots) · three men of the Ninth, read with him at the gate, near the fire at the gate end (they will not go down the Steps on his back; he has promised they will be asked; the oldest was awake all night).
- **Also in the House:** Sister Pell, keeper of the bells, whose hands were slow on the rope · the far cots, which were not read.
- **Against him:** Confessor Ivo Marrant and four grey cloaks sitting up in turns on the lower terrace, who have his name and his face; the sixth at the winch-house.
- **Next:** the day after the feast, and the questions: who was awake, which doors were barred, and what the House will believe.
- **Open choice:** none. The next comes at Chapter 2's climax.

# Characters

Voices matter more than descriptions. Each entry has a **voice** line; write their dialogue from it. Secrets are in `_gm/arc.md`.

Companion **approval** (−100 to +100) lives in `saga/state/world.json` and moves with story choices, never with real-life numbers.

---

## Ser Darrow of Edgemoor — Knight-Captain of the Ninth Lance

- **32**, tall and rangy, a horse-breeder's son from a minor house in the Edgemoor hills, raised to captain by talent rather than birth. Dark hair kept short, a broken-and-reset nose, a laugh that carries.
- Before Harrow Ford his men called him **Quickstep**. He was the fastest blade in the Lowmarch, and he fought from his feet: footwork, angles, turning a man before the man knew he'd been turned. He was proud of it, careless with it, and fed by the Grace like everyone else.
- Nineteen of his sixty came home. He remembers every name of the forty-one who didn't and says none of them aloud.
- **Now:** the leg is bound and weak, he can barely manage stairs, and for the first time in his life he has to wait. Waiting does not come naturally to him.
- **Voice:** dry, quick, deflecting with jokes; drops the jokes entirely when something matters. Calls people by their full names when he is angry. Talks to his knee under his breath, and swears at it.
- **Flaw:** he wants the old self back, and that wish is the trap. **Arc:** from wanting to be who he was to choosing who he is building, and from fighting to get back to fighting to go forward.
- **Signature:** in the Ninth, a raised fist with two fingers out meant "hold." He used to hate giving that signal.

### The Knight Who Fell (the benchmark)
The Reckoning sometimes shows Darrow, faintly, the measure he had at Harrow Ford: **Might 15 · Vigor 15 · Finesse 18 · Resolve 9**. Surpassing each of these is a story moment. Resolve is the first he will pass, and he will not notice when he does.

## Companions

### Maelis Vorne — the Mender
- Mid-fifties, small, grey-braided, scarred forearms, ink-stained fingers, a clay pipe of bitterleaf she never seems to light. Former field surgeon of three campaigns; now senior Mender at Saint Ysolde's.
- She performed the Binding. She reads it each week with two fingers and her eyes shut ("the Reading of the Knots").
- **Voice:** clinical, short sentences, no comfort she can't back up. Praise from her is rare and lands like a dropped anvil. Answers questions with exact questions. *"How does it feel?" — "Wrong question. What did it do this morning?"*
- **Rule of the saga:** Maelis never clears Darrow for anything the real-world plan has not cleared. Her verdicts track the PT's.
- **Wants:** to see one Binding hold. **Fears:** the soft season.

### Ser Hollis Garrow — the Old Captain
- 61, broad, white-bearded, loud. Knight-Captain of the Sixth Lance. At Harrow Ford he lost his right leg above the knee, and he is still learning a wooden one.
- He teaches Darrow **the Seated Blade**, the art of fighting without footwork, from a chair across a practice ring. He learned it in his own chair, recently and badly.
- **Voice:** profane, warm, a sergeant's rhythm; insults as affection ("Your Grace-fed ass"), and no tolerance for self-pity, including his own (he fails at that). Calls Darrow "Captain" to mock him, and later means it.
- **Wants:** a drink; absolution; someone to have been worth it. **Carries:** something about the order to charge.

### Wren Ashdown — the Runner
- 21, wiry, sharp-faced, ink on her hands and a pencil behind her ear. Grew up in the House after her mother, a knight, was brought up the Steps Hollowed when Wren was nine.
- Runner, ledger-keeper and resident liar of Saint Ysolde's. She counts everything: steps (1,117), bells, cots, debts. In Book I she is Darrow's legs.
- **Voice:** fast, irreverent, numbers in every third sentence, sarcasm as armor. Says "technically" a lot. Never cries where anyone can see.
- **Wants:** to know what happened to her mother. **Hides:** what she can read.
- Her ledgers supply the chapter epigraphs (see `style.md`).

### Ash — the Hound
- A grey wolfhound bitch in the House kennels with a splinted hind leg, bad-tempered with everyone except Darrow, for no reason anyone can name. She heals alongside him. She does not fetch.

### Later companions (introduced when their Book opens)
- **Ysra Tal, "the Needle of Calden"** (Book II): Oathsworn duelist, 29, precise and cold, the finest footwork Darrow has ever seen. Sent to bring him in. Becomes his Finesse mentor if he earns it. **Voice:** courteous, clipped, deadly literal.
- **Ilse of Corrach** (Book III): Thornwild war-leader, 40s, who dragged a drowning Vaelmark captain out of the Wend at Harrow Ford for reasons she has not explained. **Voice:** slow, amused, says less than she knows.

## The House of Menders (Book I cast)

- **Mother Ione** — Prior of Saint Ysolde's. 70s, gentle, iron underneath; holds the sanctuary right and will die before she yields it. Speaks in questions.
- **Brother Anselm** — the almoner, 40s, soft-spoken, beloved, keeps the stores and the letters. Always tired. Has a sister in Calden he mentions too often.
- **Sister Pell** — keeper of the bells, nearly deaf from fifty years of them, reads lips and hates fools.
- **Ser Benedek Orrin** — a wounded knight of the Sixth on the next cot; Grace-sworn, devout, kind, terrified of the Confessors and more terrified of being Faithless.
- **The far cots** — the Hollowed of years past, fed and washed by novices. One of them was Wren's mother; she died there four years ago.

## Antagonists

### Ser Aldric Vane — the Knight-Confessor
- 34, beautiful in a severe way, devout, disciplined, never raises his voice. Darrow's sworn brother: squired together, knighted the same morning, kneeling side by side. At Harrow Ford he rode at Darrow's left hand.
- Now leads the Confessors, who are charged with bringing the Faithless of Harrow Ford to the Oathspire to kneel again.
- **Voice:** courtly, patient, sincere, which is the frightening part. Writes Darrow letters that are genuinely loving. *"Come home, brother. Kneel, and be whole."*
- **Wants:** order; his sister Elinor safe; Darrow back. He is not a liar. He believes.

### Confessor Ivo Marrant — the Hound of the Oath (Book I antagonist)
- Vane's lieutenant. 40s, bald, smiling, smells of the iron censer he carries. A zealot who enjoys his work, polite to the point of menace. Never touches a weapon in front of witnesses.

### King Aurel the Evergreen
- Reigning for 311 years. Not seen in public for nine. Present in the story as bells, writs and the weight of the Oathspire. Sometimes, when Darrow is weakest, he dreams of a green-lit hall and the sound of very slow breathing.

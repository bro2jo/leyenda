# ⚠️ GM EYES ONLY — Proposals: deepening the later Books (spoilers)

*What this file is.* Plans for the plan: changes to the arc, the character notes and the engine that have been worked out against the Books as they stand but not yet applied. Each proposal says what the page already has, what changes where, what it costs, and what could go wrong. A proposal is applied at the Book opening it names (or the next chapter plan, for Book II items) and then struck from here. Nothing in this file reaches the page, the brief or the site; the build blocks its sentences like every GM file's.

## 2026-11-06 · Three devices from the research, planned against the arc

Source: the research report (the acclaimed fantasy of anime, manga and the novel) and the audit of 2026-11-05. Three of its findings the arc does not yet use: Vinland Saga's aftermath (the long reckoning after a man learns what his past was), Attack on Titan's other side (the enemy lived with before the hero reaches them), and Berserk's Eclipse rule (a loss that cannot be prevented, only priced). All three fit the arc as it is; none needs a new Book.

### A. The reckoning after the ford (Books III–V)

**What the page has.** Prologue I: the clans drawn up wrong, carts and children behind a screen of spears; the red-handed woman looking at him; he looked at the carts, then at the spears "because the spears were a line", and he loved the charge, and would be honest about that later because Maelis would make him. The Heartbeats stopped the Ninth, not his conscience. b2.5 confirms what the stones take (T1/T2). b3.2 already carries the hinge in option 1's cost: *what he saw is also what he did, and Ilse will say so in the hall.* b3.3 reveals T4/T5: the Lances were sent to cut down people fleeing the Tide, and he went gladly.

**What is missing.** The plan spends the reveal in a chapter (b3.3) and moves on to b3.4 and the Long Night. The research's finding is that the praised stretch is the aftermath: what a man does once he knows. Here it should be a through-line from b3.3 to b5.4, paid in a vow, in choices that can break it, and in the one confession the plan already holds (q5.tobin). It is also the stall shape Books III and IV lack: when the gate is slow, the reckoning is the material, a question a chapter.

**The through-line, beat by beat.**
1. **b3.3, the morning after** (tone map: "bread and the count"). Add to the Downstream: the first time he gives the two-finger fist to *himself*, no charge, before it is words. The motif (`## motifs`: the fist he hated to give) gets its turn here.
2. **New quest `q3.hearing` — The Ford Told** (optional; can run after b3.3; priority before q3.hunt when the pace line says ahead). *Hook:* the clans' council asks the Vaelmark captain, by their law of asking (a gift before the ask, *tal*), what he was sent to do at the ford and whether he went gladly. *Stage 1:* the asking, in the hall, Ilse making him look at the right thing; the clans argue about him among themselves, funny and hard, nobody lectures. *Stage 2:* his answer, the micro, axis candor_guile: 1. the true thing, "I loved it" (candor +1; `ford_owned`) · 2. "I was sent" (guile +1) · 3. say the forty-one's names instead, and nothing of himself (0; thread 11 touched) · default 1. *The verdict* (never a death, never the knee): a cost on standing, chosen by the tier's week: barred from the hunt (q3.hunt closes), or he carries the clans' dead's lamps to the water with the women, or his sword stays at the door for the Book if it does not already (`sword_yielded`). *The vow:* standing, in the hall or at the water after, in his own words, never explained: no charge at a line he has not looked at; the two-finger fist held up as the oath's form. Flag `vow_hold`. *Payoff / spine link:* the clans' standing (thornwild faction), the vow the Books test; b3.5. *Never:* T6–T8; nothing of the hand or the knee.
3. **b3.5** is the vow's first test (a wall held all night, never a charge). Option 3 (cross and burn the Tide) gets one more cost line: *breaks `vow_hold`*. The ledger entry that takes it sets `vow_broken=true` (new flag; the Downstream line: the clans heard him swear).
4. **Book IV** is the vow as drill: the stop (Anchor) is already the Book's form; name it so in `book4.tone` ("a man who swore no charge teaches two hundred men to stop one"). q4.marches (forty boys with billhooks marched toward the ford: the Ninth as it was) is the reckoning's mercy and should run if the pace allows. Cost lines added: b4.1 option 3 (march before the stragglers) *strains* the vow (a line, no flag); b4.4 option 1 (turn the host back: a charge) and option 3 (ride back alone) *break `vow_hold`*; `r.b4.hollis_lives` is untouched.
5. **Book V.** b5.2 (the memory) and q5.tobin (the whole truth "with his own name in it") are the reckoning's confession and need no change but a line: Ilse, if present, says in the Lowmarch the one clan word she gave him in the hearing, and it means the other thing now. b5.4 option 1 (finish from the turn) *breaks `vow_hold`* at the last; option 3 keeps it.
6. **The brother is right about one thing.** T3 has Vane rein back a heartbeat early because he knew; T5 makes the same turn a mercy he did not intend: the Second never charged the carts. Add to his entry (*What he is right about*): he kept sixty men out of a slaughter and cannot say so because of why. Voiced three times, never by the narrator: Ysra at q3.needle ("the Second was told to hold; everyone knew the Grace would be thin"), Elinor at q5.second ("my brother kept his Lance out of the water and will not say so"), Vane himself at b5.4 ("I turned my horse, brother. You didn't."). The reader gets the villain who is right about something; Darrow's reckoning gets its mirror.
7. **The count** (the audit's B8): `held` / `chased`, tallied through the ledger from Book III. Engine (small): `saga.py plan tally NAME [+N]` writing `plan.json → tallies`, and `tally.<name>` in the rule grammar; the brief prints Darrow's tallies in his lines; a companion names the count in a jibe before Book V (Wren: "Charges not made: four"). If the engine work waits, the GM note carries the count by hand.
8. **The stall shape** (the audit's B1, for Books III and IV): when the Knot is slow, the reckoning is the Book's spare material: a question a chapter, each with somebody changed, never the same confrontation deferred: what he owes the clans' dead; the Ninth's forty-one, who died doing it; the Second, who did not; Tobin's mother (a letter can reach a holdfast); the forty boys. Write as the `when slow` paragraph of `book3.tone` and `book4.tone`.

**Costs.** One quest (~15 lines), two flags, five cost lines on existing options, three character lines (Aldric, Ysra, Elinor), two tone paragraphs, one Downstream line; the tally is optional engine work (an hour).
**Risks.** Preaching: the vow is never explained and nobody says what it means; the hearing is the clans' argument about him, not his about himself; Ilse laughs. Overloading Book III: q3.hearing is optional and folds into b3.5's week as a scene when the gate is fast (its asking and his answer kept, the verdict in a line).

### B. The enemy from inside (one interlude a Book, from the opposition)

**Mechanism.** design §5.1: an interlude is a slot with `kind: interlude`, `pov`, `day: null`, one per chapter at most, written on a close-day after that day's scene, recorded as `chNN:interlude`. The POV person needs a character file (one may exist unnamed: the red-handed woman's did). Each Book names one enemy interlude in its `bookN.tone` under `### interlude`: who, which beat's week, what it shows (their want; what they are right about), what it must never say (truths not yet on the page), the thread it plants. The chapter that carries it carries no other interlude and no Tether scene. It is written from inside their belief (dramatic irony), never as a lecture, and it plants, never reveals.

**Per Book.**
- **II — the Needle**, the week of b2.2, before the weir scene (ch 5 already carries Rae's interlude, slot 8, so ch 6 or 7). Shows: the hunt from her side; an order to take a man alive with no reason given; two carts' tracks out of one, read and believed (b2.2's trick from the hunted side); her literalness as comedy; what she is right about: a man who will not be moved has no answer in footwork. Never: why Vane wants him alive (T3 lands at b2.4, after). File: `ysra-tal.json` (the page has "the Needle" already; her name may be hers in her own head and his at the weir). Plants thread 1 by the wording of her orders.
- **III — Aldric in Calden**, the week of b3.3 or b3.4 (T1–T3 are on the page by then, so his interior may walk round them). Shows: the Spire's cold, a king nobody has seen in nine years, Marrant's censer in a corridor, a message in his brother's words read twice (`vane_messaged`) or Ysra's silence, Elinor noticing what he is not saying, his hands cold and he does not mind. What he is right about: see A.6. Never: Elinor as the Crown's lever beyond what the page has; Ysra's fate before b3.5 (if `ysra_spared` is false, the interlude is the news of her death reaching him). File exists.
- **IV — the captain who kneels in secret** (the Book IV temptation's man; b4.2 Costly), the week of b4.2 or b4.3. Shows: a man in the host doing the dawn forms wrong, his hands cold and his men hungry, kneeling at a field-stone at night and winning a skirmish on it the next day; the fast road working. What he is right about: the levies will die for want of what one knee gives him. Never: Vane's hand on him (Book V). File: new minor character (`## Naming palette`), a cast line; the temptation recorded (`plan temptation add`) by the scene that shows the field won.
- **V — Elinor Vane**, the week of q5.second or b5.1. Shows: a brother who does not sleep, a seal she wants read aloud, the Second's captains arguing, cold hands seen by a sister; the writs with the Faithless captain's epithet nailed up by her own people. What she is right about: the realm needs the Second whole, and a civil war is what her brother is about to start. Never: Vane's choice of ground (b5.4). File: `elinor-vane.json`; she comes onto the page in Book V in any case. (If the Book prefers, Aldric on the far bank in b5.2's week instead: the frames moved by his order, the stone where he knelt beside his brother.)
- **VI — Aurel**, the week of b6.1 or b6.2, before b6.3. Shows: the green hall from inside it, the slow breathing as his own, three hundred years of bells counted, a man afraid of dying and certain the realm dies with him; a Confessor's report of a captain who will not kneel, heard as a child hears of a storm. What he is right about: the Hollowing ended by decree would be a real end. Never: the finale's options; the Stone's mechanism beyond what b2.5 and b4.2 gave. File: `king-aurel.json`, alive; "never on the page in person before Book VI" holds.

**Costs.** One slot a Book (an eighth, day null), five plan blocks under `bookN.tone`, three files when their Books come. Engine: nothing; `check` already refuses two interludes in a chapter.
**Risks.** A truth ahead of schedule: every interlude block carries its `never` line and the brief prints it. An enemy made too sympathetic too early: each is right about one thing and wrong about the hero, and the next beat shows the cost of their rightness.

### C. One fixed loss a Book (the tier prices it, never prevents it)

**The model.** b1.5: the rope cut and Pell hurt in every tier; the tier shaped who held the stair against how many. The audit found the later Books' every-tier beats are mostly things going right.

**The rule** (add to `## fates`): each Book from II has one loss that happens in every tier of its beat; the tier sets its price (what goes with it, who carries it, what it costs in standing); it is planted on the page at least a chapter before; once on the page it joins this list. Never a survivor, never the knee (design principle 4), never Rae herself (`rae.road`); a place, a thing, a standing.

**Per Book.**
- **II — the mill (b2.3)** burns in every tier. Prices: *Triumph:* every cart over, the bridge down on his word, the millers follow him with their stores · *Hard-won:* every cart over, the bridge held past dark, Darrow carried off it, the millers' stores lost · *Costly:* every cart over, mill and stores burn, nowhere to go but with him · *Setback:* as Costly, and the Hollowed cross too. Plant: the mill tracks (b2.1, slot 7) and q2.lantern's river mill. (The hand, `c04.12b`, is Book II's fate on his body; the mill is the world's loss.)
- **III — the river line (b3.3)** is lost in every tier, so the Long Night is at the holdfast wall in every tier (b3.5 already is). Prices: *Triumph:* it holds until the children are through and is given up in order · *Hard-won:* it bends all night and is left at dawn · *Costly:* the outer palisade burns · *Setback:* overrun; the Tide has the river. (Rewrite b3.3's Triumph line accordingly.)
- **IV — the ford-house (b4.4)** burns in every tier once the host is across. Prices: *Triumph:* the Sixth marches out singing before it goes · *Hard-won:* Hollis carried out of it as it goes · *Costly:* it burns with the Sixth's stores and the bridge · *Setback:* the Sixth scattered from it, not lost.
- **V — Edgemoor (b5.1):** his father's holding under a Confessor's writ, the greys taken for the Second's remounts, in every tier. Prices: *Triumph:* word by a carter, one filly kept back in the far field · *Hard-won:* he sees the brand on the Second's horses at the parley · *Costly:* the house burned · *Setback:* his father's name on the writ as a harbourer (never his death by tier). Plants: Tale 1 (the paddock, the brand, the far field) is on the page; b5.1's writs on the bell-house doors. Decide at Book V's opening; the alternative is Thistle's line, which `rae.tether` forbids.
- **VI — the cart (q6.market / b6.2):** the price of the Spire's lower door falls on Rae's cart and oxen in every tier (`r.b6.rae_door` decides whether the door opens to her word; the cart goes either way: as the price, or left at the stair's foot to whoever holds it). Prices: *Triumph:* the cart and one ox the Market's, the other ox hers · *Hard-won:* cart and oxen · *Costly:* the cart burned at the foot of the stair · *Setback:* the Second holds it and she drives it back for them. The Tether's fifth stage then means what it says: a carrier without a cart. Rae is never the price; the cart is.

**Costs.** Five per-tier rewrites, five `**Fixed:**` lines, the rule in `## fates`, two plant lines. Engine: none; the climax brief prints the beat. A `check` note ("Book N's fixed loss not on the page by its beat") can come later.
**Risks.** Grimness: each loss is a place or a thing, each is priced in the tier's words, and the chapter after it is warm (the millers' bread, the thaw camp, the host's song). Predictability: the losses are planted but never announced, and the tier's price is the surprise.

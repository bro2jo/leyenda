# Cast — appearance and voice

Reader-safe. One entry per character the Chronicle has already put on the page: how they look and how they talk, nothing else. Wants, fears, flaws, arcs, secrets and hidden sheets live in `_gm/characters.md`, and everyone not yet on the page lives there in full; companion approval lives in `saga/state/world.json`. Write every line of dialogue from the voice line. `saga/characters/<id>.json → appearance` may draw on this file, and both must agree with what the page has shown; the day a new character steps on the page, their appearance and voice move here from the GM file.

## Ser Darrow of Edgemoor
- **Appearance:** Thirty-two, tall and long-limbed, dark hair cropped short and never quite flat, a few days of dark stubble, grey-green eyes in a lean, weathered face, a nose broken once and set a little crooked with a faint scar across the bridge, a dry, lopsided smile, and a laugh that carries the length of a hall. At Saint Ysolde's he wears an infirmary shirt and breeches, often with a grey wool blanket round his shoulders, and moves with care: the good leg first, then the bad one, then the slow transfer. This week he walks without the stick, and does not limp. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/darrow.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Dry and quick, deflecting with a joke, and the jokes stop dead the moment something matters. He uses a person's full name when he is angry. He talks to the knee under his breath, and swears at it.

## Maelis Vorne
- **Appearance:** Small, somewhere in her fifties, a deeply lined, weathered face, steady grey eyes, a few old nicks of scar at the corner of her mouth and chin, grey hair pulled back into a long braid worn over one shoulder, forearms seamed with old scars, fingers stained with ink. She dresses for the cold in a worn dark coat and a frayed wool scarf. A clay pipe of bitterleaf sits clamped in her teeth and is never lit; it travels from one side of her mouth to the other when she is deciding something. She can sit for a long time with two fingers on a joint and her eyes shut. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/maelis-vorne.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** Clinical, in short sentences. She offers no comfort she cannot back up, and praise from her is rare enough to land like something heavy dropped on a floor. She answers a question with a sharper question. *"How does it feel?" "Wrong question. What did it do this morning?"*

## Ser Hollis Garrow
- **Appearance:** Sixty-one, broad through the chest and thick through the neck, a bald crown with close-cropped white hair at the sides, a full white beard, a face gone red and broken-veined from wind and drink, pale grey-blue eyes in a nest of creases, an old nick of scar through one white eyebrow, and loud enough to be heard over a river in flood. His right leg is gone above the knee since Harrow Ford; he is learning a wooden one, and hates it. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/hollis-garrow.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Profane and warm, with a sergeant's rhythm; insults are how he says he is fond of you ("your Grace-fed ass"). No patience for self-pity, his own included, at which he fails. He calls Darrow "Captain" and lets the word carry whatever he needs it to.

## Wren Ashdown
- **Appearance:** About twenty, wiry and sharp-faced, grey eyes under thick dark brows, a dusting of freckles across her nose and cheeks, a sly half-smile, brown hair twisted up out of the way and forever coming loose, ink on her fingers, a pencil behind her ear and a ledger hugged to her chest like a shield. In the cold she buries her chin in a thick dark wool scarf. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/wren-ashdown.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** Fast and irreverent, a number in every third sentence, sarcasm worn as armor. Says "technically" whenever she is about to contradict you. She does not cry where anyone can see.

## Rae Thorne
- **Appearance:** Twenty-seven, or near it, and strong through the shoulders in the way that comes from hauling and not from Grace. A fair, open face with the wind's colour in it, grey-green eyes, honey-brown hair that her hood gives up holding, so that it falls loose past her shoulders and curls in the damp. She wears a dark hooded wool cloak pinned at the throat with a plain iron clasp over a linen shirt the colour of old cream, carter's boots, and a small brass ox-bell on a cord at her belt that rings whenever the cart jolts; she never seems to hear it. She drives the last cart up the Holloway to the foot of the Thousand Steps, and she looks at a hurt leg the way a carter looks at a cracked wheel: a thing that will have to be seen to, and not by her. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/rae-thorne.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** Lowmarch plain, and spare with it. She talks to her oxen in whole sentences and to knights hardly at all; a thing is said once, and "Aye" does the work of a paragraph. The one thing she said to Darrow in a day's road: *"They'll count you up. Don't help."*

## Ser Aldric Vane
- **Appearance:** Thirty-four, beautiful in a severe way, disciplined to the bone: a pale, clean-shaven face, black hair worn loose to the collar, level dark-grey eyes that give nothing away, a thin old scar above one brow. He dresses with care, in a high-collared dark tunic trimmed in silver under tooled leather. Of the two boys who once held other men's horses together, the one with the better seat and the better manners. He never raises his voice. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/aldric-vane.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Courtly, patient and sincere, which is the unsettling part. He spends few words and leaves the one that matters unsaid.

## Tobin Marsh
- **Appearance:** Nineteen, the youngest of the Ninth Lance: shaggy light-brown hair forever falling in his eyes, hazel eyes, wind-reddened cheeks, a patchy young beard, a wide grin with a gap between his front teeth. At the ford he wore a quilted gambeson under a dark hooded cloak, a leather strap across his chest with an iron buckle; he rode at Darrow's left in the crossing. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/tobin-marsh.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** He sang, badly and without stopping. Nothing he said has been written down.

## Mother Ione
- **Appearance:** The Prior of Saint Ysolde's, in her seventies: small and very upright, white hair cropped close under a plain linen coif, a soft round face creased like a winter apple, and pale eyes that count everyone in a room in turn. The creases run all over it: rosy cheeks, sparse white brows, very pale grey-blue eyes, and a small firm mouth that keeps a faint, patient smile. The coif is tied under her chin, and she wears a high-collared gown of plain grey-brown wool under a worn grey-green wool shawl. Gentle in face and manner, with iron under the gentleness. In the warming room she has the one good chair by the fire and offers it to nobody. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/mother-ione.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** She speaks in questions, and the questions are never idle. She gives an order by asking whether someone would like to do it. *"Then the Captain should hear the law, shouldn't he? Maelis?"*

## Sister Pell
- **Appearance:** Keeper of the House's bells for fifty years, and so old she might have been cut from the hearthstone: small, wrapped in two shawls, knuckles swollen like walnuts. Her face is grey and furrowed all over, the mouth a thin flat line, a few white whiskers on the chin, pale grey eyes narrowed under sparse white brows, and wisps of white hair escaping a dark wool kerchief; the shawls are one brown and one faded grey, wrapped high. Nearly deaf from the bells, she watches a speaker's mouth rather than their eyes, and a cup chatters in her hand before it settles. Her glass stands in the bell tower. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/sister-pell.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** A dry rasp, rationed: she says what the bells have left her breath for and not a word more, and has no patience for fools. *"Fifty years I've rung the bells. Ask the bell if it's tired."*

## Ser Benedek Orrin
- **Appearance:** Not yet thirty, a knight of Hollis's own Sixth, on the cot next to his. A broad, open face that looks younger than its years: freckles across the nose and cheeks, ears that stand out a little, brown eyes under brows that lift in the middle as if an apology were always ready, thick dark-brown curls gone damp and flattened by the pillow, and a week's brown beard he has not been able to shave. A boar spear went through his shoulder at the ford and a fever came after; the fever sits red and high on his cheeks and shines at his temples, and the shoulder is wrapped like a parcel and tied with cord. He lies with his hands folded on the blanket and watches, with the eyes of a man who still feels a warmth in his chest, or says he does. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/benedek-orrin.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Soft, earnest, and unable to leave a thing alone: he asks what things mean and offers the comfort he was taught, kneeling and mercy, as if it were his to give. He says his oath at every bell and calls Hollis "Ser"; when Hollis insults him, he smiles, because that is what the words are for.

## Confessor Ivo Marrant
- **Appearance:** A Confessor in his forties in the grey cloak of his order, who puts his hood back in the cold so that his bald head is bare to the snow: a pleasant, well-fed face, round and clean-shaven, the cheeks pink with cold, pale brows that hardly show, small light-grey eyes that hold very still, a small mark on the chin, and a smile of neat, even teeth that he gives to a whole court at once. He carries the iron censer on its chain in his own hand, so that he stands in his own grey weather, and the smell of hot iron reaches a gate ahead of his voice. At the bars he held nothing but the censer and the writ. His grey cloak fastens at the throat with a plain iron toggle. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/ivo-marrant.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Soft and courteous, every sentence a door he is holding open for you. He uses a name as if he had been given it, thanks people for their candour, and says the thing he means pleasantly, to a whole court. *"Bells grow tired."* *"I'll wait. It's the thing I'm best at."*

## The red-handed woman
- **Appearance:** A clanswoman of forty or more, older than she looked from across the water: a weathered, freckled face with high cheekbones and a strong nose, amber-brown eyes under heavy brows, a scar through one eyebrow, dark hair gone grey at the temples in one long braid bound with a leather thong, a small iron ring in one ear, and hands painted red to the wrist. At the ford she wore a dark green wool mantle pinned with a bronze ring brooch over a leather jerkin and a coarse undyed tunic, with a spear over her shoulder. She has the patient, furious face of someone who has been right about something for a long time. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/red-handed-woman.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** A few words of rough Vaelish, each one an order. She says less than she knows.

## Brother Anselm
- **Appearance:** A lay brother in his forties with deep brown skin, close-cropped tight black curls gone grey at the temples, and a broad, soft face that looks tired even when it smiles: warm dark eyes with the creases of too little sleep under them, the brows drawn up at the middle in a worried, kindly line, a small uncertain smile. He wears the House's grey wool hood and robe over a cream linen collar, the worn leather strap of the letter-satchel across his chest, the stores key on a cord at his belt; he presses a hand flat to his chest when he speaks, the first two fingers of it dark with ink. His portrait (`saga/art/characters/brother-anselm.jpg`) is canon for his face.
- **Voice:** Apologetic and gentle; he apologises before he asks, trails off in the middle of a sentence, and thanks people for things they have not done yet. He mentions his sister too often.

## Mirren
- **Appearance:** Brother Anselm's younger sister: the same brown skin as her brother and a great deal of dark curling hair, worn loose. Seen only in a thin torn shift, sitting against a bare stone wall, somewhere that is not comfortable. Her portrait (`saga/art/characters/mirren.jpg`) is canon for her face.
- **Voice:** Not yet heard.
